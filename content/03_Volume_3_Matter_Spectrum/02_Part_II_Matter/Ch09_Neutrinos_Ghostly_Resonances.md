**Parametric scaling (Tier 3 order-of-magnitude statement):**
$$|d_e^{GCT}| \;\propto\; \frac{\alpha^2}{\pi^2} \cdot |J_{\rm CKM}^{\rm GCT}| \quad [e \cdot \text{cm}]$$
The dimensionful prefactor (of order $e\hbar/(m_e c)$ times further loop and mass suppressions) is not derived. Below, the scaling is referenced to $1.1 \times 10^{-29}\, e \cdot \text{cm}$ at $J_{\rm CKM}^{\rm GCT} = 10^{-3}$; that reference is the ACME II limit, used as a scale, not a derived normalization.

**Unitarity envelope (Tier 3; not constraining):**
Three-generation unitarity bounds the rephasing invariant by $|J| \leq 1/(6\sqrt{3}) \approx 0.096$. Under the reference normalization the scaling grows linearly in $J_{\rm CKM}^{\rm GCT}$, so at the unitarity maximum the envelope is $\sim 10^{-27}\, e \cdot \text{cm}$. That lies above the current experimental limits ($|d_e| < 1.1 \times 10^{-29}$ $e \cdot \text{cm}$, ACME II 2018, Andreev et al. 2018 *Nature* 562:355; $|d_e| < 4.1 \times 10^{-30}$ $e \cdot \text{cm}$, JILA 2023, Roussy et al. 2023 *Science* 381:46), so a $J$-independent envelope does not constrain the model. The value $1.1 \times 10^{-29}\, e \cdot \text{cm}$ corresponds to an assumed $J \sim 10^{-3}$ and coincides with the ACME II limit by construction; it is not an independent bound.

**Conditional magnitude estimate [Tier 3 — does NOT enter the falsification gate]:**
If $J_{\rm CKM}^{\rm GCT}$ takes a value at the candidate scale $\sim O(\phi^{-22})$ catalogued under O.7, the reference normalization gives $d_e^{GCT} \approx 3 \times 10^{-31}\, e \cdot \text{cm}$ ($\phi^{-22} = 2.525 \times 10^{-5}$). This conditional magnitude is provided for orientation only and inherits the underived prefactor. A sensitivity of $\sim 10^{-31}\, e \cdot \text{cm}$ is reached at $J_{\rm CKM}^{\rm GCT} \approx 9 \times 10^{-6}$ under the same normalization; below that the signal is indistinguishable from $J = 0$ — an O.7 sensitivity issue, not a falsification.

**Next-generation detection [Tier 3, conditional on $J_{\rm CKM}^{\rm GCT}$ and the underived prefactor]:** the conditional $\sim 3 \times 10^{-31}\, e \cdot \text{cm}$ magnitude lies an order of magnitude below the ACME III target ($\sim 3 \times 10^{-30}\, e \cdot \text{cm}$) and below the current JILA molecular-ion limit (Cairncross et al. 2017; Roussy et al. 2023). Reaching it requires electron-EDM sensitivity near $10^{-31}\, e \cdot \text{cm}$.

**Epistemic Status [Tier 3 scaling / Tier 3 magnitude pending O.7].** This estimate has two layers: (i) the **parametric scaling** ($d_e \propto J_{\rm CKM} \cdot \alpha^2/\pi^2$) is a beyond-Standard-Model loop-structure claim; (ii) the **numerical magnitude** ($\sim 3 \times 10^{-31}$ $e \cdot \text{cm}$ under the reference normalization) is Tier 3 because it depends on $J_{\rm CKM}^{\rm GCT}$ (Open Problem O.7, bundled with the QLQCD-1L research debt) and on an underived prefactor.

**Falsification.** Because neither $J_{\rm CKM}^{\rm GCT}$ (O.7) nor the dimensionful prefactor is derived, no current or near-term eEDM limit falsifies the mechanism. Under the reference normalization, a limit $|d_e| < 10^{-32}$ $e \cdot \text{cm}$ at $3\sigma$ would exclude $J_{\rm CKM}^{\rm GCT}$ at or above the candidate $\phi^{-22}$ scale. A detection in the range $10^{-31}$–$4.1 \times 10^{-30}$ $e \cdot \text{cm}$ (the upper end is the JILA 2023 limit) would be consistent with, but would not by itself establish, the GCT defect-core picture.
