**20.1 Breakdown of the Continuum**

**20.1.1 The Pixelation of the Sky**

If the vacuum is a discrete quasicrystal, there must exist a maximum frequency for phason propagation (and a limiting speed $c$). Particles with energy $E > E_{GZK}$ (the Greisen-Zatsepin-Kuzmin limit, $\sim 5 \times 10^{19}$ eV [standard physics]) are the highest-energy probes available, but they remain $\sim2\times10^{8}$ below the Planck energy, so they could probe the lattice structure only through effects accumulated over propagation, not directly.

**Hypothesis [Tier 4 — speculative]:** *Ultra-High-Energy Cosmic Rays (UHECRs) traveling along the principal axes of the icosahedral lattice would experience "super-diffusive" dispersion, while those traveling along "irrational" directions would propagate unimpeded.*

If this hypothesis held, it would imply **Anisotropic Flux**: the sky map of UHECRs would show "hot spots" and "blind spots" correlated with the projection axes of the 6D hyper-lattice relative to the Earth's galactic trajectory. No LIV coefficient, direction map, or UHECR propagation calculation has been derived for this pattern, so it is not a registered prediction.

**20.1.2 Vacuum Cherenkov Radiation [Tier 4 — speculative]**

Analogous to a jet plane creating a sonic boom, a particle moving faster than the local phase-velocity of the medium emits energy. In GCT, if a cosmic ray's Lorentz factor $\gamma$ pushes its effective group velocity against the discrete grain of the vacuum, it should emit **Phason Shock Waves** (Vacuum Cherenkov Radiation).

This radiation would manifest as an anomalous energy loss for particles above a critical threshold ($E_{crit}$), creating a sharp cutoff in the cosmic ray spectrum that is *distinct* from the GZK cutoff (which is due to CMB interaction). This requires the particle to outrun the photon phase velocity, which the photon kinematics of V2 Ch06 do not allow without a separate, underived Lorentz-violating term in the matter sector; no sign or coefficient for such a term is given.

**20.2.2 Dispersion Relations and Explicit Lorentz Invariance Violation Bounds**

Protocol H requires measuring the arrival time delay between high-energy photons and low-energy photons from distant Gamma-Ray Bursts (GRBs). The general phenomenological expansion of the photon dispersion relation in a discrete vacuum is:
$$\frac{\Delta c}{c} \approx \xi_n \left(\frac{E}{E_{Planck}}\right)^n$$
where $n=1$ (linear) and $n=2$ (quadratic) are the leading Lorentz Invariance Violation (LIV) orders.

**Observational Constraint (GRB 090510):**
Fermi/LAT and GBM observations of GRB 090510 (Abdo et al. 2009, *Nature* 462:331) bound the energy scale of linear LIV:
$$E_{QG,1} > 1.2\,E_{Planck}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad |\xi_1| \lesssim 0.8 \quad \text{[empirical bound, conservative limit; one-sided, subluminal sign]}$$
As an illustrative order of magnitude only, at the $31$ GeV energy of the highest-energy GRB 090510 photon this corresponds to $\Delta c/c \lesssim 31\,\text{GeV}/(1.2\,E_{Planck}) \approx 2\times10^{-18}$. Many discrete-spacetime theories (naive loop quantum gravity, polymer quantization) generate a linear LIV term of order unity at the Planck scale, placing them at or beyond this bound.

**Why the Linear Term Cancels in the GCT Dispersion Model (and What Remains Assumed):**
The icosahedral vacuum lattice possesses an exact **center-inversion symmetry** ($\mathbf{r} \to -\mathbf{r}$), which is an element of the full icosahedral point group $I_h$. Within the App TPF dispersion model (Theorem TPF.2), the linear correction is built from a summand odd under $\mathbf{G}\to-\mathbf{G}$, so that model's coefficient cancels. The cancellation is generic (every reciprocal module is closed under $\mathbf{G}\to-\mathbf{G}$, and Friedel's law holds for any real potential); the $I_h$ center-inversion is consistent with it but not needed, so it is not an icosahedral signature:
$$\left.\frac{\Delta c}{c}\right|_{n=1} = 0 \quad \text{(within the App TPF model; generic } \mathbf{G}\to-\mathbf{G} \text{ cancellation)}$$
Inversion symmetry alone does not forbid every $n=1$ term: a rotation-invariant correction proportional to $|\mathbf{p}|$ is inversion-even, and in effective field theory the rotation-invariant $n=1$ photon terms are CPT-odd and helicity-dependent. Excluding them requires the CPT and helicity structure of the emergent electromagnetic action, which has not been derived.
