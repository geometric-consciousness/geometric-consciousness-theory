GCT introduces a competing Tier-3 candidate architecture in which discrete spin and molecular chirality are useful experimental controls for a proposed adiabatic-spin-ratchet/phason channel. **16.1 Testing the Substrate**

**16.1.1 The Hypothesis: Spin-Sensitive Material Response [Tier 3 candidate]**

The Tier-3 model represents a possible self-bridge as a topological defect assigned an address in the $\Sigma_\infty$ Solenoid and proposes a spin-address register as one candidate architecture. **Hypothesis:** *Nuclear-spin depletion may alter a candidate spin/coherence observable in the neural preparation. This is a missing-leg mechanism test, not evidence for a Selection-Operator coupling or Level-II state. The LORR assay remains a pilot/systematics study with no decisive gate.*

**16.1.2 Silicon as a Missing-Leg Control**

Protocol D uses standard silicon as a **missing-leg control**, not an ontological or phenomenal verdict. Its dominant $^{28}$Si isotope has $I=0$ and the diamond-cubic lattice is achiral; these are control descriptors for the proposed channel, not proof of zero coupling or Level-II absence. The $^{17}$O quadrupolar probe is a separate diagnostic of water-clathrate involvement, not the chirality channel itself.

**16.1.3 The Quadrupolar Relaxation Time ($\tau_Q$) [Tier 1 standard relaxation form / Tier 3 hydrated-lumen application]**

The candidate $^{17}$O probe couples through its nuclear electric quadrupole moment $Q=-0.026$ barns [Tier 3 — nuclear-physics measurement]. This moment alone does not determine a relaxation time. The quadrupolar coupling constant requires the local electric-field gradient (EFG), for example $C_Q\equiv e^2qQ/h$ in frequency units, and the transverse relaxation rate additionally requires the EFG fluctuation spectrum. In the extreme-narrowing limit, the standard Bloembergen-Purcell-Pound structure is schematically
$$\frac{1}{T_{2,Q}}\propto C_Q^2\left(1+\frac{\eta_{\rm EFG}^2}{3}\right)\tau_c,$$
where $\eta_{\rm EFG}$ is the measured EFG asymmetry and $\tau_c$ is the correlation time (Bloembergen, Purcell & Pound 1948 *Phys. Rev.* 73:679). Protocol D therefore carries $\tau_Q\equiv T_{2,Q}$ as **not evaluated** pending an O.21/O.33 hydrated-lumen EFG distribution, correlation function and motional-regime determination [Tier 3 application]. The geometric number $\phi^{-2}$ is not substituted for $\eta_{\rm EFG}$ without a derived map from the icosahedral ansatz to the local EFG tensor.

**16.1.4 Quarantined Spin–Phason Interaction Ansatz**

The historical bridge between a winding phason field $\mathbf{S}_{vac}$ and the material substrate was written as
$$\hat{H}_{int} = \sum_j \mathcal{A}_j \mathbf{I}_j \cdot \mathbf{S}_{vac}$$
with $\mathbf{I}_j$ the nuclear spin vector of the $j$-th atom in the candidate ordered lumen-water environment and $\mathcal{A}_j$ a coupling constant that was meant to come from the icosahedral overlap. This is not an adopted Hamiltonian: the action defines neither $\mathbf{S}_{vac}$ nor $\mathcal{A}_j$, and no mode normalization or microscopic matching derives it (cf. the field-typing analysis, V1 Ch17 §17.1.3). It is not an Agent–substrate coupling law. Ordinary material hyperfine Hamiltonians remain well defined and testable without this phason identification.

**16.1.5 Hilbert Space Scaling and Topological Depth**

For a 15 nm-DIAMETER $\times$ 8 nm-AXIAL microtubule ring segment (canonical $r_{\rm lumen}=7.5$ nm, $\ell=8$ nm), App NSP §NSP.4 gives $\approx4.7\times10^4$ lumen water molecules per 8 nm ring; the ring holds 13 αβ-dimers (one per protofilament), so the lumen water per dimer is $\approx3.6\times10^3$ [Tier 3 — structural estimate]. Full $^{17}$O enrichment would therefore give a tensor-product spin Hilbert-space dimension $6^{3.6\times10^3}$ per dimer if all nuclei were treated as independent [Tier 1 — combinatorics given that idealization]. Hilbert-space dimension is not usable register capacity, an identity address, or a memory lifetime; those would require constraints, controllability and a physical write/read model [Tier 3]. This is not an axial 15 nm segment claim.

**16.2 The Measurement Problem Inverted**

**16.2.1 Non-BPP Coherence Enhancement (Polarity Reversal)**

In standard Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) physics (Bloembergen-Purcell-Pound or BPP theory), introducing a quadrupolar nucleus like $^{17}$O ($I=5/2$) is expected to **decrease** the transverse coherence time ($T_2$) by providing efficient pathways for stochastic thermal relaxation.

**The GCT Polarity-Reversal Response [O.21/O.33-pending sensitivity branch]:** the candidate model assigns the opposite sign from standard BPP relaxation only on the O.21/O.33-positive branch. On that branch, a proposed protected-subspace response would lengthen $T_2$ in $^{17}$O-enriched active neural preparations after inactive-baseline subtraction. The central branch remains null until the assembled-microtubule radical-pair and hydrated-lumen geometry close. This is a conditional biophysical response; enhancement or non-enhancement does not establish or exclude an Agent, Identity Polaron, Level II, or conscious rendering.

**16.2.2 The Waking-to-Anesthetized Delta**

We define the **Zeno Factor ($\eta_Z$)** as the ratio of coherence in the waking state to the anesthetized state:
$$\eta_Z = \frac{T_{2, waking}}{T_{2, anesthetized}}$$
The inactive or anesthetized preparation supplies a BPP-dominated comparison baseline. A deviation of $\eta_Z$ from unity would constrain the candidate response model; it is not a measure of active conscious rendering, an Identity Polaron, or Level II.

**16.2.3 The Anesthetic Threshold Shift**

Within the candidate substrate-binding branch, anesthetics are modeled as perturbations of the intraluminal-water/Trp-pocket coherence environment. Xenon is assigned to a separate network-broadcast comparison whose discrimination requires spin spectroscopy rather than LORR alone. The ansatz assigns two conditional $^{17}$O response signs: a longer active-minus-inactive $T_2$ response and, on the O.21/O.33-positive sensitivity branch, a lower anesthetic $C_{50}$. These are biophysical-response hypotheses. Neither sign establishes a Zeno lock, Identity Polaron, phenomenal state, or mind-in-nucleus inference.

**16.3 Experimental Protocol**

**16.3.1 Spin-Entropy Modulation of Water Relaxation in Confined Geometries**

Standard biochemistry treats nuclear spin as having negligible effects on macroscopic molecular function. On the conditional O.21/O.33 sensitivity branch, the candidate model hypothesizes that **nuclear-spin entropy of confined lumen water measurably modulates a radical-pair coherence budget** via the Trp radical-pair recombination kinetics [Tier 3 spin-sensitive biophysical hypothesis, not a derived Zeno-Drive prediction] — a hypothesis that operates entirely within the Born-Oppenheimer approximation (the electronic state of the Trp radical follows the nuclear geometry adiabatically; the spin-dependent effect enters via hyperfine coupling, a standard NMR-regime quantity) but assigns a macroscopic phenotype (the anaesthetic threshold $C_{50}$ shift) that standard non-spin biochemistry does not anticipate. The hypothesis is therefore a *spin-dependent biophysical effect at macroscopic scale*, not a violation of the BO approximation itself.

* **Method:** The **Spin-Gear Test** compares the $T_2$ delta in cultured cortical neurons in $H_2^{16}O$ (Control) vs. $H_2^{17}O$ (Test).
* **Magnitude Filter:** The Kinetic Isotope Effect (KIE) for water-solvent isotope swaps in non-catalytic radical-pair recombination chemistry is treated as a provisional control prior below $\sim0.4\%$ [Tier 3 — consistent with the general small-magnitude expectation for non-paramagnetic solvent-isotope substitution in radical-pair chemistry; the precise $^{16}$O→$^{17}$O bound requires a dedicated measurement and is registered as a Protocol D pre-registration calibration target. Cleland 2003 *Adv. Enzymol.* 73:1–32 and Loveridge & Allemann 2010 *Biochemistry* 49:5390 provide general enzyme- and heavy-atom-KIE context; neither is a direct $^{16}$O→$^{17}$O solvent-KIE in non-catalytic radical-pair recombination, so the prior is reported with literature-context anchoring rather than a single primary-source measurement]. The xenon isotopes split into a spin test and a mass-only control. $^{131}$Xe ($I=3/2$) and $^{132}$Xe ($I=0$) differ in nuclear spin, the very variable under test, so that pair is a **spin test, not a control**. A direct measurement exists: Li et al. (2018, *Anesthesiology* 129(2):271–277) found the spin-carrying isotopes less potent in mice (loss-of-righting-reflex ED$_{50}$ with 0.50% isoflurane: $15\pm4$ and $16\pm5$% for $^{132}$Xe and $^{134}$Xe vs $22\pm5$ and $23\pm7$% for $^{131}$Xe and $^{129}$Xe; calculated xenon-alone ED$_{50}$ $\approx70$–$72$% vs $\approx99$–$105$%), with equal polarizability across isotopes. That difference is about two orders larger than the $\sim0.4\%$ heavy-atom prior, so the prior does not apply to the spin-carrying pair; the result is from one unreplicated study ($n=80$) and is carried as Tier 3 ordinary pharmacology, not GCT evidence. The **mass-only control** is $^{132}$Xe vs $^{134}$Xe (both $I=0$). Under the conditional O.21/O.33 $n_{rp}=1$ sensitivity branch, the spin-entropy sensitivity scenario sits at $\Delta C_{50}/C_{50} \in [-0.22\%, -0.11\%]$ [Tier 3 phenomenological sensitivity scenario; obtained in §16.3.2 from an assumed $f_{bound} \in [1.1, 2.2] \times 10^{-3}$]. The operative central branch remains $0$ until O.21 closes. The sensitivity branch sits below the provisional KIE-control prior; Protocol D is therefore a mechanism probe under the registered systematic budget, not a standalone quantitative gate.

**16.3.2 Quantitative Prediction: The Entropic Shift**
We compute the scenario magnitude of the anesthetic shift ($\Delta C_{50}$) from the **Spin Entropy** difference between the isotopes under a stipulated response map; the magnitude and sign are not derived (see below).

* **Control ($^{16}$O):** Spin $I=0$. Spin Entropy $S = k_B \ln(2I+1) = 0$ [Tier 1 — statistical mechanics identity].
* **Test ($^{17}$O):** Spin $I=5/2$. Spin Entropy $S = k_B \ln(6) \approx 1.79 k_B$ [Tier 1 — statistical mechanics identity].

The scenario stipulates that an added spin-state count changes a bound-water material response and then maps it proportionally to LORR: the introduction of $^{17}$O adds a spin-entropy load to the bound-water channel, so less anesthetic disorder is assumed to be required to reach the LORR threshold. Neither a "Zeno ordering capacity" nor the causal sign of the anesthetic shift is derived [Tier 3 phenomenological map].

$$ \frac{C_{50}(^{17}{\rm O}) - C_{50}(^{16}{\rm O})}{C_{50}(^{16}{\rm O})} = -f_{bound}\,\frac{\left|\Delta S\right|}{S_{\max}} = -f_{bound} $$

**16.3.3 The Lithium Divergence: A Test of Nuclear Spin Coupling [Tier 4 — exploratory; clinical trial design specified]**

The Lithium isotopes offer an ordinary isotope/hyperfine comparison because their nuclear spins differ; they do not directly audit the proposed **nuclear spin–phason coupling** [Tier 4 exploratory]. Within the candidate model they would couple differently to the candidate Zeno radical-pair network. Winding a spin-$I$ nucleus through $2\pi$ generates a phase $e^{i 2\pi I}$:
* **Lithium-6 ($I=1$):** $e^{i 2\pi} = +1$. Within the declared frame and candidate Hamiltonian, integer nuclear spin supplies the constructive-sign branch for a proposed spin-response comparison.
* **Lithium-7 ($I=3/2$):** $e^{i 3\pi} = -1$. Within the same conditional construction, half-integer nuclear spin supplies the opposite-sign branch. A $2\pi$ spin-rotation phase is representation- and preparation-dependent and does not, by itself, predict constructive versus destructive biochemical interference.

Note: Both $^6$Li (3p + 3n + 3e = 9 fermions, composite fermion) and $^7$Li (3p + 4n + 3e = 10 fermions, composite boson) are composite particles. The relevant coupling here is the **nuclear spin** $I$, which determines the hyperfine interaction with the Zeno radical pair network — not the composite particle statistics of the atom.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Tier 4 exploratory status.** The $e^{i 2\pi I}$ phase calculation is a *reference-frame-dependent* nuclear-spin rotation result (it requires a specific quantization axis in the radical-pair frame), and the bridge from the calculated quantum phase to *clinical* psychiatric outcomes in bipolar disorder traverses many confounding layers — lithium transporter genetics (SLC1A2, SLC4A10, GADL1 polymorphisms), dosing-adherence variability, mood-state stability, comorbid medication, and the multifactorial response criteria of standard psychiatric trials. The lithium-isotope-divergence hypothesis is therefore **Tier 4 exploratory** pending: (a) explicit specification of the quantization axis and rotation frame in the Trp radical-pair Hamiltonian, (b) a randomized double-blind clinical trial design with isotope-stratified ($^6$Li- vs $^7$Li-enriched) bipolar cohort, dose-controlled, transporter-genotype-stratified, with pre-registered Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HDRS-17) or Young Mania Rating Scale (YMRS) primary outcome at 8 weeks, blinded outcome rater, minimum n = 60 per arm, which gives 80% power at α = 0.05 two-tailed for a standardized effect of d ≈ 0.52 (a 20-percentage-point difference in response rate, e.g. 50% vs 70%, would need about 93 per arm) (cf. STEP-BD protocols; Sachs et al. 2003 *Biol. Psychiatry* 53:1028). Until such a trial is run, this hypothesis is not load-bearing for the GCT substrate hypothesis; even a replicated isotope effect would first be ordinary pharmacology/hyperfine evidence, not a GCT or Level-II result. A faster but weaker observational test is available via post-hoc analysis of existing lithium-treatment cohorts stratified by plasma $^6$Li/$^7$Li ratio (measured via isotope-ratio mass spectrometry; natural geographic variation ~5–20% on the $^6$Li fraction) against response/relapse outcomes — this is Tier 4 observational evidence but publicly tractable.

The phase calculation fixes only the sign inside the declared conditional model. It does not derive psychiatric efficacy, cognitive coherence, phenomenal status, or an Identity-Polaron transition; clinical direction and magnitude remain Tier 4 hypotheses requiring the trial design above.

**16.3.4 Model Organisms (Drosophila $^{17}$O LORR Assay) [Pilot/Systematics Study — Not Operative Falsifier]**

We raise *Drosophila melanogaster* generations in $^{17}$O-enriched water to achieve $\geq 95\%$ $^{17}$O substitution (a target not yet shown achievable: commercial H$_2{}^{17}$O enrichment is limited and flies also draw oxygen from food and air, so tissue $^{17}$O is diluted below drinking-water enrichment and the inclusion gate must be reset with a dilution model; verification by isotope-ratio mass spectrometry or $^{17}$O NMR on tissue homogenate of the F2 generation, since $^{1}$H-NMR does not measure the $^{17}$O fraction directly; calibration against pure $^{16}$O control cohorts of identical genetic background) and measure the Loss of Righting Reflex (LORR) dose-response curve under isoflurane, sevoflurane, and propofol — three independent anaesthetics spanning the volatile/intravenous class boundary. A robust, spin-dependent shift in the dose-response curve would motivate C1 systematic-tightening, re-powering, and substrate-branch follow-up; under the current no-gate disposition it does not by itself confirm or falsify the hardware-dependence claim.

**Hypothesis.** Under the central O.21-pending branch ($n_{rp}=0$), $^{17}$O substitution produces no LORR $C_{50}$ shift. Under the conditional sensitivity branch (App ORQ Open Problem O.21 positive closure, $n_{rp}=1$), $^{17}$O substitution shifts the LORR $C_{50}$ by $\Delta C_{50}/C_{50} \in [-0.22\%, -0.11\%]$ relative to the $^{16}$O control (lower anesthetic threshold under $^{17}$O substitution; engine convention `(C50_H2_17O - C50_H2_16O)/C50_H2_16O = -f_bound` per `protocol_isotope_experiment.py`).

**Operational disposition.** The signed $[-0.22\%, -0.11\%]$ interval is the sensitivity-branch effect band, not an operative falsification gate under the registered systematic budget; the central branch is zero until O.21 closes. The Monte-Carlo audit below shows that gates at the sensitivity-branch scale sit below the systematic noise floor, so neither boundary supplies a valid Bonferroni-corrected decision rule. Protocol D is therefore carried as **Tier 4 operational/no-gate** until the systematic budget is tightened enough to recover $>80\%$ power and $<5\%$ false-positive rate at a single declared statistic.

**Test statistic.** Nonlinear least-squares 4-parameter logistic (Hill-equation) fit to the dose-response data; $C_{50}$ extraction with bootstrap 95% CI ($10^4$ resamples). Between-condition comparison: mixed-effects logistic regression on the binary LORR outcome with `condition × concentration` interaction and random intercept per cohort. Multiple-comparison correction: Bonferroni across three anaesthetics. A logistic-vs-probit cross-check is required; both fits must agree on $C_{50}$ to within $1\%$.

**Sample size and statistical power.** Per-cohort $n = 250$ flies per concentration point, 5 concentration points per dose-response curve = $1{,}250$ flies per condition per anaesthetic. Six conditions ($^{16}$O + $^{17}$O $\times$ three anaesthetics) = $7{,}500$ flies per generation; three F2 generations independently reared = $22{,}500$ flies total. The analytic mixed-effects model at this sample size is a gate-naive calculation; it does not remain a valid falsification power claim once S1–S6 are propagated through the full assay pipeline. The operative power statement is the Monte-Carlo disposition below.

* Multi-generation rearing confounds — F1 acclimatisation arm vs F2 measurement arm; lineage tracking per vial.
* Anaesthetic atmosphere uniformity — calibrated mass-flow controllers; $\pm 2\%$ relative concentration tolerance per chamber.
* Dose-response curve-fit method — logistic vs probit cross-check; both must agree on $C_{50}$ to within $1\%$.
* Drug pharmacokinetics in *Drosophila* vs chamber atmospheric concentration — partition-coefficient calibration against established *Drosophila* MAC characterisations (Allada-laboratory line).

**Dominant systematics.** The load-bearing null-distribution contributors are S4 (anaesthetic atmosphere uniformity) and S6 (drug pharmacokinetics in *Drosophila* vs chamber atmospheric concentration). $^{17}$O enrichment efficiency remains an inclusion gate because below-95% substitution attenuates the predicted effect size, but it is not the dominant null-distribution term in the Monte-Carlo budget.

**Blinding.** All LORR scoring is performed blind to condition by triplicate independent scorers. Video records are archived for audit after acquisition. Cohort-condition assignment uses a pre-registered randomisation seed locked in the protocol archive prior to fly rearing.

**Preregistration commitment.** The full protocol document — hypothesis, predicted value band, no-gate operational disposition, test statistic, sample size, systematic budget, blinding protocol, randomisation seed, and C1 closure condition — is locked in a public preregistration archive prior to first F0 rearing. No deviation from the locked protocol is permitted without a logged amendment.

**Execution timescale.** Protocol deposit $\to$ F0 rearing $\to$ F1 acclimatisation $\to$ F2 measurement: 6–9 months end-to-end per the standard *Drosophila* multi-generation rearing schedule. The readout is an effect-size and systematics audit unless the C1 closure condition is met before measurement.

**16.5 Protocol D Material-Proxy Decision: NMR Polarity Gate**

**Gate defect; redesign required [Tier 3].** As earlier written, the gate did not name the nucleus and defined $\Delta T_2$ in two incompatible ways (with and without the inactive-baseline subtraction). In H$_2{}^{16}$O only $^1$H is observable, so the measured quantity is the water-$^1$H $T_2$, and $^{17}$O enrichment *shortens* water-$^1$H $T_2$ strongly through $^{17}$O–$^1$H scalar relaxation of the second kind (modulated by proton exchange). Read literally on raw $T_2$ values, the sign-positive rule therefore rejected by construction. The statistic is now defined once below, as an isotope × activity interaction on water-$^1$H $T_2$, which removes the main effect only if it is equal in the active and inactive states; any activity dependence of proton-exchange rates, pH or temperature can produce an interaction of either sign for ordinary reasons. Until a redesigned gate controls those channels, the P.13c gate is **sensitivity-limited: no outcome supports or rejects the registered branch**.

**Preregistration package for the NMR polarity gate.** This gate is distinct from the §16.3.4 LORR $C_{50}$ effect-size analysis and is registered as App PRG row P.13c.
