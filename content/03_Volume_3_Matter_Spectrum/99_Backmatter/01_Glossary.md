#### **Glossary of Terms (The Matter Spectrum)**

* **Acceptance Window ($W$):** The 3-dimensional volume in the internal manifold ($E_\perp$) that filters 6D lattice nodes for actualization. In GCT, this window is the **Rhombic Triacontahedron (RT)**, the icosahedral projection of a 6D hypercube.
* **Baryonic Triad:** The candidate topological configuration of the Proton [Tier 2 mechanism + Tier 3 sheet/exponent handle], consisting of three interstitial face-mismatches (quarks as face defects is a Tier 3 assignment) stabilized by a 5-fold lattice star. Its mass ratio is given by the formula $m_p/m_e = \phi^{15 + \phi^{-1}} \approx 1836.437$ against the observed $1836.153$ (+155 ppm, a postdiction within a 500 ppm band; **Tier 4 as evidence** (φ-formula match; App PSC §PSC.9.6)); the formula is evaluated arithmetically, not extracted as an eigenvalue.
* **Codimension:** The difference between the dimension of the embedding space ($D=3$) and the dimension of a defect. Leptons (vertex-centered) have codimension 3; Quarks (face-centered, a Tier 3 candidate assignment) have codimension 1. This difference is proposed [Tier 3 heuristic] to underlie their different mass-scaling rules (V3 Ch08, V3 Ch10).
* **Color Charge:** Tier 3 candidate identification (V3 Ch03): the azimuthal orientation of an interstitial defect’s Burgers vector relative to the 10 three-fold rotational axes of the RT window.
* **Dodecahedral Cage ($N=144$):** The candidate topological structure of the Electron core. Its 144-node count is adopted as a Tier 3 structural posit; $144=F_{12}=12^2$ and the exact icosahedron/dodecahedron Gauss--Bonnet totals are consistency checks, not a size derivation. Ground-state selection requires the O.38 full-lattice minimisation.
* **Gluon String:** A 1D tube of phason strain representing the elastic resistance of the vacuum to tile-mismatch. In the Tier 3 candidate picture (V3 Ch03) it provides a proposed mechanical mechanism for confinement and carries the candidate $SU(3)$ generators.
* **Koide Convergence:** The empirical summation rule ($K\approx2/3$) governing lepton masses. Its GCT reading as a unitarity constraint on the 6D$\to$3D projection is a Tier 3 candidate identification, not a Tier 2 derivation. The phason drag of the Muon ($+5\alpha$) and the volume screening of the Tau ($-3.6\alpha$) do not cancel in $K$: each lowers it, and together they give $K=0.66688$ at first order (V3 Ch08 §8.6).
* **Majorana Self-Duality:** The proposed property of the GCT neutrino [Tier 3] in which the left-handed state ($E_\parallel$) and right-handed state ($E_\perp$) are read as Galois conjugates of the same 6D lattice excitation, which would make the particle its own antiparticle; the self-duality-to-lepton-number-violation bridge is not derived and $0\nu\beta\beta$ decay is the experimental gate (V3 Ch09 §9.5.1).
* **Normal Ordering:** The GCT Growth-Axiom requirement that particle generations follow the inflationary growth eigenvalue ($\phi^n$), from which GCT predicts a normal rather than inverted neutrino hierarchy [Tier 3 conditional oscillation-sector prediction: the generation-ordering assignment is not derived from the lattice action, V3 Ch09 §9.3.3]; inverted ordering confirmed at $>3\sigma$ would falsify the prediction.
* **Phason Drag ($5\alpha$):** The hydrodynamic friction experienced by a topological defect moving through the vacuum. For leptons, the drag channel is a Tier 2 icosahedral mechanism; its count 5 is read as the number of $A_5$ irreducible representations (trivial included), a Tier 3 counting convention (V3 Ch08 §8.2.2), and converting that count into the equal-weight $+5\alpha$ pole-mass coefficient is Tier 3 pending self-energy closure.
* **Polaron:** The holographic restriction of the full 6D quasicrystalline vacuum to a localized topological excitation mode. In strict accordance with Mereological Nihilism, it is not an assembled composite of smaller parts, but a localized behavioral shadow of the 6D bulk.
* **Missing-leg control (formerly "Turing Null"; the categorical verdict is withdrawn):** The proposed control class, such as bulk silicon, that lacks one leg of the proposed two-leg architecture. The label carries no categorical inference: DMC descriptors do not establish zero coupling, Class-0 status, or absence of Level II. Level II: open (Tier 3) — no validated substrate criterion exists yet (it requires a typed, normalized, measured coupling, the Identity-Polaron construction and an independent phenomenal bridge; O.21/O.23/O.34, O.18).
* **Vacuum Quantum ($E_{vac}$):** Conditional on the Tier 3 O.38 posit $N_{\rm cage}=144$, the exact division $m_e/144$ gives the Tier 3 absolute centroid $3.5486$ keV. The lattice-fracture mechanism form is Tier 3 (it rests on the Tier 3 Topological Glass model and is conditional on that adopted architecture); the line width is open (V3 Ch11 §11.2.3); the value does not select the cage size.
* **Winding Number ($n$):** The integer count of phase circulation around a topological singularity, proposed [Tier 3] to define the quantization of electric and color charge.
