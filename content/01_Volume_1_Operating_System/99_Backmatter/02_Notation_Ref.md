### **Notation Quick Reference**

#### **Fundamental Spaces**
* **$\Psi$:** The Global Wavefunction (The Field).
* **$\Sigma_\infty$:** The Adelic Solenoid, GCT's candidate address-and-resolution space ("Identity Tree" only after a filtration is chosen; the root is then the whole level-zero cylinder, not an owner or subject).
* **$E_\parallel$:** Parallel Space (The 3D Physical Projection).
* **$E_\perp$:** Perpendicular Space (The 3D Internal Manifold).
* **$\mathcal{P}$:** The Total Configuration Space; $\Sigma_\infty \times (E_\parallel \oplus E_\perp)$ is a Tier 3 factor ansatz, not a canonical product theorem.

#### **Operators and Generators**
* **$\mathcal{F}_{sel}$:** The Selection Operator (The Focus): a formal non-unitary Agent-frame update. No examined physical carrier, instrument, clock, intent channel, owner or phenomenal bridge realizes it; it is not a Solenoid transition.
* **$\hat{\mathcal{R}}$:** The proposed Realization Operator (The Renderer); its identification with a physical render of experience is an open Tier 3 bridge.
* **$\hat{H}$:** The Hamiltonian Constraint (The Law of Zero).

#### **Metrics and Distances**
* **$d_H$:** Hierarchical (p-adic) Distance: the common-cylinder ultrametric on a declared filtration; address resolution only, not physical distance, scope, ownership, reachability or memory.
* **$\kappa_{ij}$:** Coupling Coefficient between Agents $i$ and $j$: a Tier 3 phenomenological physical coupling kernel; a common address prefix is insufficient.
* **$\lambda_c$:** Phenomenological Consensus Decay Constant in the optional ansatz $\kappa_{ij} = \kappa_0 e^{-\lambda_c d_H}$ (exponential suppression of inter-Agent coupling with p-adic distance); the filtration, address-to-coupling law, functional form and value require physical selection or calibration and do not follow from Solenoid topology.
* **$K_\parallel, K_\perp$:** Phonon and Phason Stiffness constants.

#### **Coordinate Conventions**
* **$n$:** Sequence Index (formal Agent Frame; an ordinal selection count, not a derived physical or phenomenal clock).
* **$\tau_{coord}$:** Lattice Coordinate (Field Frame; scalar address).
* **$\mathbb{I}_K$:** Proposed Hierarchical Identity Address (intrinsically an all-prime residue tuple; a single p-adic expansion is a chosen projection).
* **Disambiguation:** $\tau_{coord}$ refers to the relational coordinate; $\tau$ is reserved for the Tau lepton; $\tau_Q$ (with subscript $Q$ always explicit) denotes the quadrupole shielding time.

#### **GCT Fundamental Constants**
* **$\phi = (1+\sqrt{5})/2 \approx 1.6180$:** The Golden Ratio — the central organizing invariant of GCT.
* **$\xi \approx 7.25$ nm:** The Polaron Healing Length: $\xi = \hbar c / (\alpha^2 m_e c^2) = a_0/\alpha \approx 7.25$ nm [Tier 1 arithmetic; its identification as the vacuum/polaron healing length is Tier 3; Tier 3 microtubule-lumen biological-scale match, App DPS §DPS.5]. * **$\eta_{stiff} = \phi^{-18}$:** The Stiffness Ratio ($K_\perp / K_\parallel$) [Tier 2 integer-identification via the $H_3$ Shephard-Todd invariant-degree sum $\{2,6,10\} = 18$, unique within the finite irreducible rank-three classification but also attained by reducible $A_1\times I_2(14)$ and hence conditional on the irreducible icosahedral premise + Tier 3 physical-link conjecture (Parameter Ledger P3b, listed outside the postulate count like H-EW), App DPS §DPS.4; first-principles RG derivation of the $\phi^{-D}$ running pending Open Problem O.15(b)]. Disambiguates from $\eta_g$ (glass order parameter, V2 Ch11) and $\eta_{visc}$ (fluid viscosity, V2 Ch13).
* **$\eta_g$:** Glass Order Parameter ($0 \leq \eta_g \leq 1$) [V2 Ch11]. Distinct from $\eta_{stiff}$ and $\eta_{visc}$.
* **$\eta_{Zeno}$:** Conditional external-rate ratio $g_{single}^{eff}\sqrt{N_{bio}}/\kappa$. Its numerical scenarios do not derive the coupling from material descriptors and supply no Level-II verdict. Distinct from $\eta_{stiff}$, $\eta_g$, and $\eta_{visc}$.
