**11.3 The Consensus Action Functional**

**11.3.1 Definition and Terms of the Action**
The following is a **candidate variational model**, not a derived consensus law. It defines a Consensus Action $\mathcal{J}[\Phi]$ that penalizes deviation from a chosen set of local descriptions $\{\Phi_j\}$:

$$ \mathcal{J}[\Phi] = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{j} \kappa_{ij} \int |\Phi(x) - \Phi_j(x)|^2 dx + \lambda S_{entropy}[\Phi] $$

* **First Term:** The "Tension" between the local consensus and individual agents.
* **Second Term:** The entropic cost of maintaining the structure.

**11.3.2 Derivation of the Weighted Mean**
The Euler-Lagrange equation $\frac{\delta \mathcal{J}}{\delta \Phi} = 0$ retains both terms of the action:
$$ \sum_j \kappa_{ij} \left( \Phi(x) - \Phi_j(x) \right) + \lambda \frac{\delta S_{entropy}}{\delta \Phi(x)} = 0 $$
Solving for the local potential:
$$ \Phi_{local}(x) = \frac{\sum_j \kappa_{ij} \Phi_j(x) - \lambda \, \delta S_{entropy}/\delta \Phi(x)}{\sum_j \kappa_{ij}} $$
The entropic term enters as a correction suppressed by the total coupling weight $\sum_j \kappa_{ij}$. Because $\delta S_{entropy}/\delta \Phi$ itself depends on $\Phi$, this is an implicit equation, not a closed solution. Wherever the entropic functional is stationary ($\delta S_{entropy}/\delta \Phi = 0$), in the $\lambda \to 0$ limit, or wherever $\sum_j \kappa_{ij}$ is large for reasons independent of redundancy (larger $R$ raises $\lambda_c$ and so *lowers* $\kappa_{ij}$, §11.2.4), the stationarity condition reduces to the pure **Weighted Mean**:
$$ \Phi_{local} = \frac{\sum_j \kappa_{ij} \Phi_j}{\sum_j \kappa_{ij}} $$
It is this limiting form whose fixed-point convergence is established by Perron-Frobenius analysis in App ORQ §ORQ.1. [Tier 1 — variational calculus given the §11.3.1 action; the reduction conditions are stated explicitly]

Given the assumed action, its stationary point is a minimum when $\mathcal J$ is convex (e.g. $\lambda \le 0$ for a concave $S_{entropy}$); otherwise it need not be. Identifying that mathematical stationary point with objective reality or a multi-agent phenomenal network is a Tier 3 interpretation.

**11.3.3 The Consensus Lock**
Within the assumed action, a larger aggregate coupling weight changes the cost of deviations. This does not yet explain external-world rigidity, imply billions of active Selection Operators, or establish an infinite energy requirement; those claims are withdrawn pending a physical derivation.

**11.3.4 Fluidity from Low $\mathcal{J}$**
Applying the candidate action to dreaming is Tier 4 speculative. Dream phenomenology does not currently validate a private potential or a restructured local manifold.
