### **Chapter 9: The Rendering Engine**

**9.1 Simulation Ontology**

**9.1.1 Beyond Passive Observation**

Standard quantum mechanics is often described in terms of a passive observer and collapse. In a timeless Wheeler--DeWitt setting, GCT proposes an **Active Rendering Ontology**: a structural correlation between an address-like coordinate $\mathbb{I}_K$ and a local restriction of the Field $\Psi$ to an entanglement wedge. The interpretation of that correlation as a particular subject's experienced physical reality is an additional **Tier 3 psychophysical bridge**, not a consequence of the Wheeler--DeWitt constraint or of solenoidal topology alone.

**9.1.2 Reality as an Autopoietic System**

GCT uses **autopoietic** as a model description: the proposed Realization Operator satisfies $\hat{\mathcal{R}} \circ \hat{\mathcal{R}} = \hat{\mathcal{R}}$ at the level of an idempotent projection. Applying that proposed operator to an already-realized subspace leaves that subspace unchanged. This algebraic property does not by itself establish self-actualization, computation, or phenomenal rendering; those interpretations remain **Tier 3 candidate bridges**.

The framework proposes cross-perspectival consistency constraints. A coordinate in the solenoidal construction can label a local geometric restriction $E_\parallel$; treating that label as a subject, and treating unselected structure as phenomenally unrendered, are separate **Tier 3** commitments. Ordinary dynamical and decoherence accounts remain available for the stability of shared records.

**9.1.3 The Holographic Simulation Model**

The physical world is modelled as a **cut-and-project shadow**, sometimes loosely called "holographic": a lower-dimensional projection ($3D$) containing the encoded information of a higher-dimensional source ($6D$). This is not holography in the technical holographic-principle sense, where a boundary encodes a bulk with area-law entropy [Tier 3 metaphor]. 
* **The Source:** The $6D$ Hyper-Lattice $\mathbb{Z}^6$ (The geometric substrate).
* **The Projection:** The $3D$ Parallel Space $E_\parallel$ (The Screen).
* **The Rendering:** a proposed Agent-relative reading of the geometric output of the Realization Operator. Its identification with first-person phenomenal experience is a Tier 3 Russellian bridge, not an output of the projection mathematics.

This model offers one candidate account of why a mathematically represented system may be described as phenomenally tangible. The move from the $6D$ source and projection mathematics to tangibility for an owner of experience is not yet derived.

**9.4.3 The No-Signalling Constraint**

No-signalling does not follow from address topology. It follows conditionally from the outcome-averaged CPTP identity below, which presupposes Born-weighted outcomes (see the constraint after the box), and, in GCT's field description, from the conditional phason--phonon readout bound of Appendix SNE §SNE.4 (subluminal only on the extra Tier 3 premise $v_{\rm phonon}\le c$, which fails under P3b; App ORQ O.48). Shared cylinders alone establish no correlation or channel.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Proposition: Outcome-averaged no-signalling identity. [Tier 1 QI identity; conditional GCT application in Appendix FTH §FTH.4 and Appendix SNE §SNE.4]**
> Conditioning on a specified outcome at subsystem A does not by itself change the *unconditional* density matrix $\rho_B$ of a spatially separated subsystem B, and so does not transmit a signal. Two statements must be distinguished. (i) *Selective description:* conditioning on A's specific outcome yields the conditional state $\rho_{B|\text{sel}} = \text{Tr}_A\!\big[(\hat{P}_\text{sel} \otimes \hat{I}_B)\,|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|\,(\hat{P}_\text{sel} \otimes \hat{I}_B)\big]/p_\text{sel}$, with $p_\text{sel} = \langle\Psi|\,\hat{P}_\text{sel} \otimes \hat{I}_B\,|\Psi\rangle$. This conditional state generally differs from $\rho_B$; the difference encodes pre-existing correlation, not a transmitted signal, and is inaccessible to B without classical communication of A's outcome. (ii) *Non-selective description:* B's local statistics, unconditioned on A's outcome, are governed by the outcome-averaged map. For any complete orthogonal family of local projectors $\{\hat{P}_k\}$ on $\mathcal{H}_A$ with $\sum_k \hat{P}_k = \hat{I}_A$,
> $$\sum_k \text{Tr}_A\!\big[(\hat{P}_k \otimes \hat{I}_B)\,|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|\,(\hat{P}_k \otimes \hat{I}_B)\big] = \text{Tr}_A\!\big[|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|\big] = \rho_B,$$
> by cyclicity of the partial trace over $\mathcal{H}_A$, idempotency $\hat{P}_k^2 = \hat{P}_k$, and completeness. It is this outcome-averaged equality — not a Selection-Operator theory and not the selective map alone — that supplies the standard quantum-information no-signalling result. Correlated records can later be compared only through ordinary causal interaction when future light cones overlap (the conditional multi-Agent model of Chapter 11). Appendix SNE §SNE.4 additionally gives a **conditional** subluminal response for a declared strain-level phason--phonon source; GCT has not derived a physical $\mathcal{F}_{sel}$ event, its coupling to that source, or a phenomenal Consensus mechanism.

**Constraint on biased selection [Tier 1 — standard QI consequence of the identity above; its application to GCT selection is the open problem App ORQ O.48].** The identity sums the *unnormalised* projections, so it holds when A's outcomes occur with the Born weights $p_k=\text{Tr}\big[(\hat{P}_k\otimes\hat{I}_B)\,|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|\big]$. If a will-, resonance- or impedance-biased Selection Operator (§9.5.3; Ch10 §10.3.2) reweighted A's outcomes to $q_k\neq p_k$, B's outcome-averaged state would become $\sum_k q_k\,\rho_{B|k}$, which in general differs from $\rho_B$: the bias would be a superluminal signal (Gisin 1990; Polchinski 1991). Any selection bias GCT entertains must therefore leave the outcome-averaged marginals at their Born values on every subsystem. Local unitary or CPTP steering before an outcome is registered is compatible with this, because it changes the state to which the Born rule applies rather than the rule. The manuscript does not yet state a mechanism that biases individual outcomes while meeting the constraint; the gap is registered as App ORQ O.48.

* **Topological-friction hypothesis:** A large mismatch is proposed to impose an energetic or statistical cost. Its Hamiltonian, coefficient, and branch-filtering law remain to be derived.
