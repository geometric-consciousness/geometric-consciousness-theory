### **Volume 1 Notation and Symbolic Conventions**

To ensure precision in our derivations and avoid symbolic collisions with the particle physics definitions in Volume 3, we adopt the following symbolic conventions for Volume 1.

#### **Fundamental Spaces**
* **$\Psi$ (The Field):** The Universal Wavefunction satisfying $\hat{H}\Psi=0$. The fundamental substrate of consciousness. Where the text says "consciousness field $\Psi$", it means the Field, in its Level I, non-subjective sense.
* **$\Sigma_\infty$ (Adelic Solenoid):** GCT's candidate address-and-resolution scaffold for Identity, constructed as the inverse limit of circle wrappings, with all-prime transversal $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}\cong\prod_p\mathbb{Z}_p$ [Tier 1 mathematics]. It has no intrinsic single-prime filtration, rooted tree, owner, memory, history, transition law, or phenomenal interpretation; a chosen cylinder tree lives on a selected base fiber or local transversal chart. Treating it as the fiber that encodes an observer's internal state is a Tier 3 bridge requiring a physical address map.
* **$\mathcal{P}$ (Configuration Space):** The domain of the consciousness field $\Psi$. The shorthand $\mathcal{P} \cong \Sigma_\infty \times (E_\parallel \oplus E_\perp)$ is a **Tier 3 factor ansatz**, not a topology theorem or a literal physical realization; using a Solenoid coordinate in a physical render additionally requires a physical address map and a physical realization rule.
* **$E_\parallel$ (Parallel Space):** The 3-dimensional physical manifold (the "screen" of projection).
* **$E_\perp$ (Perpendicular Space):** The 3-dimensional internal manifold containing the "hidden" degrees of freedom (Phasons).
* **$\Phi_0$ (Vacuum Condensate):** The specific ground-state lattice geometry (Supersolid Quasicrystal) that solves the Hamiltonian constraint. Distinguished from $\Phi$ (Higgs Field).

#### **Operators and Generators**
* **$\mathcal{F}_{sel}$ (Selection Operator):** A proposed linear, non-unitary Agent-frame update (conditionalization) on declared states, intended to represent conscious actualization by mapping the global superposition to a specific experiential section. No examined physical carrier, instrument, clock, intent map, owner, or phenomenal event presently realizes it, and $\Sigma_\infty$ supplies no dynamics for it.
* **$\hat{\mathcal{R}}$ (Realization Operator):** The proposed Agent-relative realization/projection rule relating Field data to a render in $E_\parallel$. Treating a $\Sigma_\infty$ address as one of its inputs requires a physical address map, and the physical address-to-render rule itself is an open Tier 3 bridge.
* **$\hat{H}$ (Hamiltonian Constraint):** The generator of time reparameterizations that annihilates the physical state ($\hat{H}\Psi=0$).

#### **Metrics and Coordinates**
* **$\mathbb{I}_K$ (Hierarchical Identity):** The proposed transversal residue coordinate for the observer, extracted only after a physical address map and a base-fiber/local-chart choice. It is a compatible all-prime residue system in $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$ (a full Solenoid point also carries its suspension coordinate); a single $p$-adic digit string and its substrate realization are selected Tier 3 data.
* **$\tau_{\text{coord}}$ (Relational Time Coordinate):** The ordinal address of a configuration in the static lattice sequence. *Distinct from the Tau lepton ($\tau$) appearing in Volume 3.*

> **Note:** $\tau_{coord}$ denotes coordinate time exclusively. The symbol $\tau$ without subscript refers to $\tau_{coord}$. The quadrupole shielding time uses $\tau_Q$ (subscript $Q$ always explicit). The tau lepton mass uses $m_\tau$.

* **$d_H(i,j)$ (Hierarchical Distance):** A compatible ultrametric on a declared nested-cylinder filtration of the identity address tree. It measures common address resolution, not physical scope or ownership.
* **$\kappa_{ij}$ (Coupling Coefficient):** A Tier 3 phenomenological physical interaction kernel between agents $i$ and $j$, sometimes modeled as $\kappa_0 e^{-\lambda_c d_H}$ only after a filtration, a physical address map, and an independently justified address-to-coupling law are declared.
* **$\lambda_c$ (Consensus Decay Constant):** A phenomenological range parameter in the optional exponential coupling ansatz. Neither its value nor the exponential law is determined by Solenoid topology; both require calibration or derivation from the physical kernel.

#### **Temporal Language Conventions**
Because GCT treats time as emergent, we distinguish between two frames of reference:
* **Agent Frame (Sequential):** A conditional description indexed by an ordinal $n$ and using terms like "next," "then," "evolution," and "process." Identifying it with an observer's perspective or with phenomenal time requires the open transition, clock, owner, and phenomenal bridges.
* **Field Frame (Timeless):** The perspective of the global wavefunction. Uses terms like "static," "correlation," "geometry," and "structure." Time is a coordinate ($\tau_{coord}$).

#### **The Tier Classification System**
We categorize all claims by their epistemic status:
* **Tier 0 (Bedrock / First-Person Datum):** Exactly one item: experience is occurring, i.e. there is presence now. It is neither postulated nor derived; no other claim may enter Tier 0. Axiom 1 remains its stipulated universal Level I generalisation (Tier 1). Tier 0 supplies no Level II substrate verdict; those remain open (Tier 3).
* **Tier 1 (Structural Axiom):** Results following necessarily from axioms + mathematics.
* **Tier 1/2 (Architectural Postulate + Conditional Mathematics):** Results that follow from the specific architecture choice (6D lattice, icosahedral projection) but are not deductively forced by the axioms alone. The architecture is justified by parsimony and empirical adequacy, not logical necessity, and conditional mathematics never promotes the premise it assumes. Marker: `[Tier 1/2]`
* **Tier 2 (Geometric / Derived):** Results following from the specific icosahedral quasicrystal ansatz.
* **Tier 3 (Phenomenological / Calibrated):** Models calibrated to observation where first-principles derivation is incomplete.
* **Tier 4 (Speculative / Order-of-Magnitude):** Exploratory calculations where dimensional structure is correct but numerical coefficient is uncertain by >1 OOM. Claims include explicit OOM bounds. Tier 4 also covers ontological extensions outside the core falsifiable theory. Example: biogenic dark energy driving force (V2 Ch14).
