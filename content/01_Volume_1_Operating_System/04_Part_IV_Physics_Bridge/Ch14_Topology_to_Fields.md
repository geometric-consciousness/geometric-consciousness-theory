We must show how discrete, point-like "particles" are the effective descriptions of stable topological restrictions of the global Field $\Psi$, viewed from the macroscopic Agent Frame.

The proposed solution is **Soliton-Field Duality**. GCT posits that the "particles" of physics are not fundamental entities, but rather the **Collective Coordinates** of topological defects within the vacuum lattice [Tier 3 physical realization]. Appendix CCD §CCD.1 explicitly **assumes** a stable static defect profile; it does not prove the existence or stability of a finite-energy soliton in the Appendix ULA action. The proposed mapping to observed particle quantum numbers (Volume 3, Chapter 1) is a separate GCT construction and cannot substitute for the missing existence/stability derivation. QFT is the proposed **Effective Language** for these solitons when viewed from the macroscopic Agent Frame.

**14.2 The Collective Coordinate Method [Tier 1 conditional method; Tier 3 GCT realization]**

**14.2.1 Ansatz: Soliton Decomposition**

**14.2.2 Bare Mass vs. Physical Mass**

This decomposition reveals the two components of particle mass:
1. **Bare Mass ($m_{\text{bare}}$):** The integrated energy density of the static profile $\Phi_0$. It represents the minimum work required to "tie" the knot in the lattice.
2. **Radiative Correction / Phason Drag:** The energy contribution of the fluctuation field $\delta\Psi$. In the proposed physical interpretation, a moving knot creates a "wake" in the phason fluid whose self-interaction may correct the bare mass (**Phason Drag**) [Tier 3]. Any coefficient calculation or lepton-ratio comparison (Volume 3) must therefore be established independently; Appendix CCD is not evidence for either.

Within this conditional model, **Physical Mass** is written as the sum of these effects: $m_{\text{phys}} = m_{\text{bare}} + \delta m(\delta\Psi)$.
