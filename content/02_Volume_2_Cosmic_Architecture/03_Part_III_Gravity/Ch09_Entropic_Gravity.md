$$\alpha_{grav} = \frac{G \cdot m_p^2}{\hbar c} = \left( \frac{m_p}{M_{Planck}} \right)^2$$
Because $m_p/M_{Planck}\approx7.7\times10^{-20}$, $\alpha_{grav}\approx5.9\times10^{-39}$, about $8\times10^{-37}$ of the electromagnetic $\alpha$ for the same pair (the often-quoted "$10^{40}$" depends on which particles are compared). [Tier 1 arithmetic]

In this reading gravity is weak because **matter's characteristic mass scale sits far below the Planck scale of a rigid metric**: a single defect (the proton) is $\sim10^{19}$ times less energetic than the lattice bond scale. This restates the hierarchy as the small input ratio $m_p/M_P$; it does not explain why that ratio is small, and neither $m_p$ nor $M_P$ is derived here from the lattice without the Tier 3 anchors of Volume 3 (cf. V1 Ch13 §13.1.3).

**9.3.3 Interpretive Gloss: Rigidity and Responsiveness [Tier 4 — interpretive]**

As an interpretive gloss, the hierarchy can be read as expressing two features of the proposed medium: a rigid vacuum (large $M_P$) that supports stable structure, and soft phason modes (small $K_\perp$) that carry light and, in the author's interpretive programme, the conditions for structured experience. On a simulation-style reading of the cosmos, the ratio would then describe a medium that is both **Objective** (hard metric) and **Responsive** (soft information), and gravity's weakness could be pictured as a "Cost of Objectivity". This gloss is Tier 4. The ratio $m_p/M_P$ is an input (§9.3.1), the soft-phason premise rests on the Tier 3 stiffness map P3b, and no requirement of consciousness, light or simulation is shown to fix the numerical hierarchy. The physics of this chapter neither derives the gloss nor rules it out (cf. Ch04 §4.2.4).
