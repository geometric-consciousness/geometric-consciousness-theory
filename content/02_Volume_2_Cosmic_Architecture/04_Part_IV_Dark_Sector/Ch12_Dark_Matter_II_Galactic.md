### **Chapter 12: Dark Matter II (Galactic Dynamics)**

The **Radial Acceleration Relation (RAR)** is the observed tight relation between total and baryonic acceleration in galaxies. Geometric Consciousness Theory (GCT) treats a non-linear **Topological Glass** response as a Tier 3 candidate account of that relation, not as an established physical origin or a completed derivation. This chapter states the material-response ansatz, derives its mathematical consequences conditionally, and records the missing field equations, normalization, and empirical closure.

**12.1 The Elastic Regime**

**12.1.1 Solid vs. Fluid Gravity**

In standard General Relativity, spacetime is modeled as a pseudo-Riemannian manifold rather than as a material solid with a shear modulus. GCT's supersolid-quasicrystal ontology motivates a candidate non-zero phason stiffness $K_\perp$ and corresponding effective **Shear Modulus ($\mu_\perp$)**. Applying that microscopic ontology as a galactic constitutive law is Tier 3 and remains to be derived.

The Tier 3 ansatz assigns the high-acceleration regime to the usual metric response and posits an additional **Elastic Restoring Force** in the low-acceleration regime. Conditional on this unclosed constitutive model, the effective interaction is written in two components:
1. **Metric Refraction:** The density and velocity gradients of the condensate (Newtonian/Einsteinian gravity).
2. **Lattice Tension:** The non-linear elastic resistance of the topological glass to being distorted by mass defects.

**12.1.2 The Elastic Restoring Force in the Quasicrystal**

The candidate model assumes that a localized galactic mass defect induces a long-range, pinned phason-strain field in $E_\perp$. In its **Quasi-Static Approximation**, that field is represented as a **Geometric Spring** contributing to the orbital acceleration. The required galactic-scale phason equation, source coupling, and boundary conditions have not yet been derived, so the tether interpretation is Tier 3 rather than an established physical mechanism.

**12.1.3 Non-Linear Potential in the Low-Acceleration Limit**

The Tier 3 constitutive ansatz assumes that the vacuum glass becomes non-linear in the low-acceleration regime and that its effective response approaches a $1/r$ force, equivalently a logarithmic potential. Conditional on that assumed response, flat rotation curves follow mathematically. The manuscript does not yet derive the strain-stiffening law or establish it as the physical origin of the observed curves.

**12.2 MOND Emergence Ansatz [Tier 3 pending hydrodynamic derivation]**

**12.3 The Critical Acceleration ($a_0$)**

**12.3.1 Numerical Value: $a_0 \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-10}$ m/s² [Tier 3 — empirical normalisation]**

The "Deep-MOND" regime is parameterized by the empirical acceleration constant $a_0$. GCT proposes a Tier 3 material interpretation; $a_0$ is not presently derived from the microscopic model.

**12.3.2 Interpretation: Boundary Condition from the Hubble Radius**

The candidate interpretation associates $a_0$ with the scale at which the phason correlation length reaches the **Causal Horizon**. This horizon boundary condition and its normalization are Tier 3 assumptions.

**12.3.3 The Material-Cosmological Link: $a_0 \sim cH_0$ [dimensional scale $a_{\rm th}=cH_0$ (bookkeeping); low-acceleration mechanism Tier 3 (V2 Ch09 §9.1.6); Tier 3 empirical normalisation and open coefficient]**

$$a_0 \approx \frac{c H_0}{2\pi}$$
where:
* **$c$:** The phason **signal velocity** (speed of light).
* **$H_0$:** The cosmic expansion rate (Hubble constant).

Using the canonical $H_0=67.4\ {\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}=2.18428523\times10^{-18}\ {\rm s^{-1}}$ and exact $c=299792458\ {\rm m\,s^{-1}}$ gives
$$cH_0=6.54832237\times10^{-10}\ {\rm m\,s^{-2}},\qquad \frac{cH_0}{a_0}=5.45694,$$

$$\frac{cH_0}{2\pi}=1.04219787\times10^{-10}\ {\rm m\,s^{-2}},$$

This order-of-magnitude proximity motivates the material-cosmological comparison, but the $1/(2\pi)$ coefficient, the physical normalisation, and the balance interpretation remain Tier 3/open. GCT does not currently derive $a_0$.

**12.4 Galaxy Rotation Curves**

**12.4.1 Inner Region (Newtonian Regime) [Tier 2]**

For $a \gg a_0$ (near the galactic center), the refractive acoustic metric dominates. The Newtonian force ($1/r^2$) is much stronger than the elastic tension of the lattice. Gravity follows the standard laws of General Relativity, and the orbital velocities $v = \sqrt{GM/r}$ [Tier 1] track the visible mass distribution exactly.

**12.4.2 Outer Region (MOND/Elastic Regime) [Tier 3 candidate response]**

For $a \ll a_0$ (at galactic outskirts), the Tier 3 ansatz assigns the additional response to an **Elastic Regime**. If the non-linear phason strain has the assumed constant-tension form, then $a \approx \sqrt{a_0 a_N}$ and flat rotation curves follow as conditional mathematical consequences. The physical response and its normalization remain open.

**12.4.3 RAR Constraint on the Two-Component Ansatz**

GCT treats the **Radial Acceleration Relation** as a constraint on its two-component dark-sector ansatz, not as evidence that either component's physical origin or normalization is quantitatively closed. The Tier 3 model allows a primordial frozen-strain background without baryons (V2 Ch11 §11.2.1) and assigns a separate candidate baryon-induced elastic response to track the visible-mass distribution. In RAR-tested low-acceleration systems ($g_{\rm bar}\lesssim10^{-11}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$), the baryon-independent primordial contribution must therefore remain subdominant within the observed scatter: the total log-residual budget is $\leq0.13$ dex, with the intrinsic scatter statistically consistent with zero after observational uncertainties are accounted for (McGaugh, Lelli & Schombert 2016; Lelli et al. 2017). Separate field equations, relative amplitudes, and the regime boundary are not supplied here. Open Problem O.46 makes this a quantitative falsification gate: any decomposition that requires a primordial residual above $0.13$ dex in that regime, or produces a statistically non-zero intrinsic-scatter term, fails the current two-component formulation.
