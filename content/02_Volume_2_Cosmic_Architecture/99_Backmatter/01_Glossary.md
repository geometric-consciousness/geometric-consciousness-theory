## **BACK MATTER**

### **Glossary of Terms (The Cosmic Architecture)**

#### **Lattice & Projection**
* **6D Lattice ($\mathbb{Z}^6$):** The fundamental Euclidean hypercubic lattice of the parent space. It serves as the static coordinate backbone of the Operating System, possessing a lattice constant $a_6 = 2\ell_P$, twice the Planck length.
* **Acceptance Window ($W$):** A specific geometric volume in the internal perpendicular space ($E_\perp$) that acts as a selection filter. A lattice point in 6D is actualized in the physical 3D world if and only if its internal projection falls within $W$. In the icosahedral vacuum, $W$ is a Rhombic Triacontahedron.
* **Parallel Space ($E_\parallel$):** The 3-dimensional physical manifold we inhabit, representing the "Screen" of the projection. It is the arena of standard matter, forces, and the acoustic metric of gravity.
* **Perpendicular Space ($E_\perp$):** The 3-dimensional internal manifold orthogonal to physical space, representing the "Sink" of the projection. It hosts the phason degrees of freedom and, in the open Tier 3 colour candidate, color charges, and is proposed as an entropy reservoir for the universe (the Tier 4 Entropy Sequestration Hypothesis, Ch07 §7.2).
* **Cut-and-Project:** The mathematical and physical process by which a high-dimensional periodic lattice is sliced at an irrational angle to generate a lower-dimensional aperiodic structure (a quasicrystal).

#### **Elastic Properties**
* **Phonon:** A "hard" vibrational mode involving the compression or shearing of the 6D lattice bonds in the physical manifold ($E_\parallel$). Phonon stiffness ($K_\parallel$) is anchored to the Planck scale.
* **Phason:** A "soft" topological mode involving the rearrangement of the lattice tiling through the internal manifold ($E_\perp$). GCT assigns phasons a proposed role in the electromagnetic field; their qualitative distinction from phonons is structural within the supersolid model, but their numerical stiffness ($K_\perp$) is not fixed by the projector.
* **Stiffness Hierarchy ($K_\perp \ll K_\parallel$):** The candidate material hierarchy of the vacuum that would supply the vast energy gap between the Planck scale and the scale of light and standard forces. The registered value $K_\perp/K_\parallel=\phi^{-18}$ is the Tier 3 stiffness-map hypothesis P3b (pending O.15(b)), listed outside the postulate count like H-EW; only the $H_3$ integer $D=18$ is Tier 2 within GCT.
* **Lubensky-Ramaswamy Formalism:** The hydrodynamic framework for icosahedral quasicrystals used in GCT to describe the coupling between phason strain, phonon displacement, and superfluid phase.

#### **Hydrodynamics**
* **Superfluid Velocity ($\mathbf{v}_s$):** The gradient of the vacuum condensate phase ($\theta$), representing an irrotational superfluid current; its reading as the probability current of the consciousness field is a Tier 3 interpretive identification.
* **Phason Strain ($w_{ij}$):** The spatial gradient of the internal topological displacement ($\mathbf{u}_\perp$). It represents the local "tilt" of the acceptance window and is proposed as the material source of the vector potential $\mathbf{A}$ (Tier 3 declared open programme, V2 Ch06 §6.1).
* **Second Sound:** A propagating wave mode in the supersolid vacuum involving the out-of-phase oscillation of the lattice nodes and the superfluid phase. GCT proposes identifying Second Sound with the physical photon (Postulate 6.1; Tier 3 declared open programme, V2 Ch06 §6.1).
* **Healing Length ($\xi$):** The characteristic distance over which the vacuum order parameter recovers from a disruption. In GCT's biological application, the operative polaron scale is the Bohr–Compton combination $\xi = \hbar c/(\alpha^2 m_e c^2) = a_0/\alpha \approx 7.25$ nm [Tier 1 arithmetic; its identification as the vacuum/polaron healing length is Tier 3]; its microtubule-lumen match is a Tier 3 biological-scale match (Ch03 §3.2.1).

#### **Gravity & Spacetime**
* **Acoustic Metric:** The effective Lorentzian geometry ($g_{\mu\nu}$) experienced by lattice excitations (whether the gravitational carrier sector is phonon or phason is open, Ch04 §4.1.1). In the analogue model, gravitational kinematics appear as the refraction of these waves by gradients in the vacuum density and stiffness [Tier 2 within the declared model]; the Einstein dynamics enter only through the Jacobson construction of Ch09.
* **Entropic Gravity:** A horizon/screen thermodynamic construction relating entropy gradients, temperature and effective gravitational dynamics (Jacobson; Verlinde; Ch09). Reading it as a pressure that aligns the Selection Operator with regions of maximum bit-density is a Tier 3 analogy, not part of the gravity derivation.
* **Gravastar:** A non-singular alternative to a black hole consisting of a liquid core of Symmetric-Phase vacuum supported by negative potential pressure, bounded by a thin shell of crystalline lattice [Tier 4 model (declared open programme); the mass-dependent core density it needs is an open item, Ch10 §10.2.1].
* **Transparency Mechanism:** The proposed Diophantine protection afforded by the irrational $\phi$-projection, intended to keep the discrete lattice "invisible" to high-energy cosmic rays. Within the App TPF scattering model the inversion-odd $n=1$ Lorentz-violation coefficient cancels by centre-inversion symmetry; excluding the remaining rotation-invariant $n=1$ terms requires the Tier 3 CPT/helicity assumption on the emergent electromagnetic action (V2 Ch08 §8.3.5). Linear-order Lorentz invariance is therefore conditional on that assumption, not ensured.

#### **Dark Sector**
* **Topological Glass:** The metastable state of the vacuum characterized by frozen phason strain and disordered tile orientations. In the Tier 3 topological-glass model this state is proposed to supply the "invisible mass" of galactic halos.
* **Glass Order Parameter ($\eta_g$):** A scalar field describing the degree of configurational pinning in the vacuum lattice, where $\eta_g=1$ is a perfect crystal and $\eta_g=0$ is a fractured fluid.
* **MOND Emergence:** A Tier 3 constitutive ansatz in which a non-linear vacuum shear response has MOND-like mathematical limits. Flat rotation curves follow conditionally from the assumed response; its microscopic physical adoption and normalisation remain open.
* **Critical Acceleration ($a_0$):** The empirical MOND normalisation $a_0\approx1.2\times10^{-10}$ m/s$^2$ [Tier 3 input in GCT]. The Tier 3 low-acceleration thermodynamic step (Ch09 §9.1.6, Step 5) uses $H_0=67.4$ km/s/Mpc and supplies only the scale relation $a_{\rm th}=cH_0$, after the imported Bekenstein–Hawking normalisation removes a counting factor of 4. Numerically, $a_{\rm th}=6.54832237\times10^{-10}$ m/s$^2$, so $cH_0/a_0=5.45694$, not $2\pi$. The candidate coefficient $cH_0/(2\pi)=1.04219787\times10^{-10}$ m/s$^2$ has residual $-13.1502\%$ relative to $a_0$; it is a Tier 3/open normalisation comparison, not a derivation.
* **Biogenic Dark Energy:** A Tier 3 phenomenological correlation (a channel-shape fit) between a stipulated biological information-history variable and a small dark-energy perturbation. Reading biological information processing and dark energy as co-phenomena coupled to the phason field is a Tier 4 physical-source interpretation; no collective Selection Operator, physical bit-generation source or microscopic stress-energy coupling is presently derived. Self-organized criticality is a candidate amplification mechanism; the causal energy budget and absolute amplitude remain open (Ch14 §14.3.1–§14.3.2; self-organized criticality in §14.4.1; App PSC §PSC.6).
* **Dark Energy Lagrangian:** The Tier 3 phenomenological action coupling the local bit-generation rate density ($\mathfrak{i}$) to the relaxation of the scalar phason potential ($\phi_\perp$); the physical source map is not derived. As written it cannot produce $w<-1$ without a ghost, so its phantom direction is Tier 4 (declared open programme, Ch14 §14.3.1).
* **Phantom Phase:** The sustained deviation of the equation of state into $w(z) < -1$ [phantom direction Tier 4: no ghost-free action producing it is derived, Ch14 §14.3.1]. In the stipulated biogenic-DE template it is modelled as occurring when a supplied complexity-growth history accelerates faster than the lattice relaxes; this does not establish a physical biogenic source (Tier 4). The IMP-01 pipeline ($w(z)$ asymptotes to $-1$ from below, never literally crossing — see Ch14 §14.6.3) carries the registered (stipulated) Class-2 template envelope $|w + 1| \in [2,5]\times10^{-5}$ near $z \approx 0.28$; the $10^{-3}$ scale is only a broad logistic comparison branch. Observational distinguishability therefore requires covariance-aware Roman/Stage-V low-$z$ precision below $5\times10^{-5}$, and the colloquial "phantom crossing" term refers to a thresholded phantom-side deviation, not a mathematical zero of $w + 1$.
