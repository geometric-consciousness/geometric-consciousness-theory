This reference complements the Volume 1 Notation Reference and the global Parameter Ledger. Symbols below are those most heavily used in V2 (Crystallography, Light, Gravity, Dark Sector).

#### **Lattice and Manifolds**
* **$\mathbb{Z}^6$:** The 6D parent integer lattice (Crystallography Ch01–Ch03).
* **$H_3$, $H_4$:** Coxeter root systems associated with the icosahedral cut.
* **$E_8$:** The maximal exceptional Lie root system, used in the conditional McKay ($2I\to E_8$) and Elser–Sloane ($E_8\to H_4$) correspondences (V2 Ch01 §1.2.1). It is not the parent of the 6D lattice: $D_6\subset\mathbb{Z}^6$, and $\mathbb{Z}^6$ has norm-1 vectors and does not embed in the even lattice $E_8$.
* **$E_\parallel$, $E_\perp$:** Parallel (physical 3D) and perpendicular (internal 3D) projections of the 6D lattice.
* **$\Sigma_\infty$:** The Adelic Solenoid (V1 Ch07), an inverse-limit address/resolution space. Volume 2 does not use it in its physical constructions. Its interpretation as a physical identity carrier is Tier 3 and requires an independent physical address map, which the acoustic-metric constructions of Volume 2 do not supply.
* **$W$ (Acceptance Window):** Rhombic Triacontahedron (RT) — the cut-and-project filter in $E_\perp$.
