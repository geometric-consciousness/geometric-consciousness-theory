### **Appendix FTH: Foundational Theorems**

**FTH.1 The Dimensionality of Space (D=3): The Knot-Carrier Conditional [Tier 1 knot theorem; Tier 3 GCT carrier premise]**

The Axiom of Intelligibility (Chapter 2) motivates persistent structure. GCT then **postulates**, for this argument, that one candidate physical carrier of a persistent label is a closed one-dimensional defect whose ambient-isotopy class matters. This is a physical-carrier premise, not a theorem that identity addresses or memories must be knots.

One-dimensional filaments (flux tubes) are the simplest defects that admit ordinary knotting. The theorem below identifies the unique Euclidean ambient dimension for non-trivial classical knot types of embedded circles; the inference to physical space is conditional on the carrier premise and on a separate storage dynamics.

**Theorem FTH.1 (The Euclidean Classical-Knot Bound):**
*For smooth embeddings $S^1\hookrightarrow\mathbb{R}^D$ with the usual ambient-isotopy equivalence, non-trivial classical knot types occur exactly at $D=3$.*

**Proof:**
1. **Lower Bound ($D\leq2$):** For $D=1$ the case is vacuous: there is no embedding $S^1\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^1$ (no continuous injection of the circle into the line exists). For $D=2$ an embedded circle has no over/under crossing structure, and by the Schoenflies theorem every such embedding is isotopic to the standard circle, so there is no non-trivial classical knot type. This statement does not rule out other labels on non-simply-connected ambient manifolds; those are outside the theorem's Euclidean scope.
2. **Upper Bound (D $\ge$ 4):** In four or more Euclidean dimensions there is sufficient "room" to untie any 1D knot. In codimension at least three, the embedding space $\operatorname{Emb}(S^1,\mathbb R^D)$ is path-connected, so its component set $\pi_0$ is a singleton: every smooth loop embedding is isotopic to the unknot. This is a statement about path components, not a claim that the full embedding space is simply connected.
3. **The critical dimension (D=3):** The codimension of a 1D string ($3-1=2$) allows braiding while preventing strands from passing through one another, so $D=3$ supplies infinitely many distinct mathematical knot types. Turning those types into a physical information store requires an order parameter, read/write map, permitted-process class, energy barriers, noise dynamics and boundary conditions; topology alone does not make a carrier thermally or materially non-erasable [Tier 1 topology; Tier 3 storage bridge].

**Conclusion:** Conditional on using ordinary one-dimensional knot type as the carrier, $D=3$ is the unique Euclidean dimension with non-trivial classical knot classes. Topology supplies candidate labels only. Physical storage still requires an order parameter, state map, read/write coupling, allowed-process class, energy functional, noise model, boundaries and persistence time; the theorem alone does not force $D=3$ from identity or make a knot non-erasable [Tier 1 topology; Tier 3 GCT carrier/storage bridge].

**FTH.2 The Metric Signature (Euclidean Stability Ansatz) [conditional: Tier 1 under its stated premise; the signature identification is a Tier 3 architectural assumption]**

Relativistic spacetime carries Lorentzian signature, while an elastic material configuration commonly uses a positive-definite spatial/material metric. GCT adopts the latter for the parent lattice $\Lambda$ and asks whether the declared elastic energy is bounded below.

**Proposition FTH.2 (conditional):** *Given a static parent-lattice energy modeled as a positive quadratic elastic functional, a positive-definite Euclidean material metric supplies a bounded ground state. Identifying that material metric with the complete parent-space signature is a GCT architectural assumption.*

**Proof:**
Consider the energy functional for the 6D lattice $\mathbb{Z}^6$.
1. **Indefinite quadratic control:** If the same $G_{IJ}$ is inserted directly into a quadratic elastic energy and has negative directions, that quadratic form is not bounded below without further constraints or stabilizing terms.
2. **Static equilibrium:** A positive-definite elastic Hessian is sufficient for a stable local ground state of the declared static lattice model. It does not by itself create a topological index, a preserved sector, a finite decay barrier, or particle identity.
3. **Result:** The chosen parent-lattice **material metric** is Euclidean within this model. The argument neither excludes emergent Lorentzian spacetime nor proves that every possible stable microscopic action must use this ansatz. The Agent-frame interpretation of time is a separate Tier 3 claim (Chapter 16).

**FTH.3 The Rank-Six Icosahedral Module and the Distinct $E_8$ Anchor [Tier 2]**

Why rank 6? It is the minimal integral module carrying the full three-dimensional icosahedral representation and its Galois-conjugate partner.

**Theorem FTH.3 [Tier 2 — depends on the icosahedral ansatz; see Appendix USS §USS.7]:** *The minimal rank of an integral module supporting the full three-dimensional icosahedral representation is 6. This rank statement does not select a unique Bravais lattice.*

**Proof (Galois Degree Argument):**
Adopt the icosahedral point group $H_3$ for the vacuum (App USS Hypothesis H1 and Lemma III; Parameter Ledger postulate P1). Isotropy motivates this choice but does not force it. This group involves rotations by the Golden Ratio $\phi$. However, a discrete lattice requires symmetry operations with **Integer Traces**. To rationalize $\phi$, we must utilize its Galois extension field $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$, which has a degree of 2. The minimal embedding dimension is the product of the physical dimension and the degree of the algebraic extension: $3 \times 2 = 6$.

*Note on tier status:* This argument derives the **rank-six requirement** from the icosahedral ansatz — it does **not** independently establish why icosahedral symmetry was selected or choose a six-dimensional Bravais centring. The icosahedral selection is treated separately in Appendix USS (Lemma III). Together, Lemma III + Theorem FTH.3 give: (a) conditional adoption of the icosahedral group [Tier 1/2, App USS Lemma III]; (b) the $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt5)$ character field [Tier 2 under that ansatz]; and (c) a rank-six integral module for the full three-dimensional icosahedral representation. The Bravais alternatives are primitive $P\cong\mathbb Z^6$, face-centred $F\cong D_6$, and body-centred $I\cong D_6^*$; GCT's primitive-$P$ choice is an additional Tier 1/2 architectural adoption. Appendix USS §USS.7 states the distinct McKay $2I\to E_8$, Elser–Sloane $E_8\to H_4$, and rank-six/Bravais-classification steps without conflating them.

**The distinct $E_8$ connection:** Under McKay, $2I$ corresponds to $\widetilde E_8$, while the Elser–Sloane orthogonal projection maps the 240 $E_8$ roots to two concentric $H_4$ 600-cell shells with radius ratio $\phi$. These closed facts organize the conditional architecture but do not turn the adopted $P\cong\mathbb Z^6$ lattice into a sublattice or uniquely selected projection of $E_8$.

**FTH.4 The No-Signalling Proposition [Tier 1 selection rule; Tier 3 subluminality premise]**

**Proposition FTH.4 (Conditional Subluminal Physical Readout):** *Choose an address filtration and suppose a separately derived physical law associates apertures $i$ and $j$ with a common cylinder in $\Sigma_\infty$ and a measurable phason-source coupling. Any disturbance read out in $E_\parallel$ through the strain-level $K_3$ phason–phonon channel propagates no faster than the phonon speed $v_{\rm phonon}$, and therefore no faster than $c$ under the additional premise $v_{\rm phonon}\leq c$ [Tier 3 premise; it fails under the P3b speed map].*

*Proof.* The conditional argument is given in Appendix SNE §SNE.4. Icosahedral character theory forbids displacement-level phonon–phason mixing but permits exactly one invariant strain-level coupling (the $K_3$ / $C^{mix}$ term of quasicrystal elasticity; V2 Ch05 §5.2.2). Once a declared phason source drives that $K_3$ channel, the induced physical disturbance is carried by the phonon propagator at characteristic speed $v_{\rm phonon}$. The bound $v_{\rm phonon}\leq c$ is an extra premise, not a GCT result: the book identifies $c$ with the phason (second-sound) speed (V2 Ch06 Postulate 6.1; Parameter Ledger row $c$), and under the Tier 3 stiffness-map hypothesis P3b the reference phonon speed is $v_\parallel\approx\phi^{9}c>c$ (App TPS §TPS.1); under P3b the premise fails and the readout is bounded only by $v_{\rm phonon}$. Sharing a chosen Solenoid cylinder is only a finite-resolution address relation: it does not place two apertures at one point, establish a correlation, or supply an access channel. This conditional field-propagation statement is distinct from the standard quantum-information no-signalling theorem for completely-positive trace-preserving maps; see Appendix SNE §SNE.4 for the complete conditional argument and its scope. $\square$

**FTH.5 Conditional Galois/Gap-Label Candidate Space [Tier 1 algebra conditional on the adopted model; Tier 3 physical adoption]**

**Proposition FTH.5 (conditional arithmetic range):** *For observables constructed from the adopted icosahedral cut-and-project coordinates and the specified Fibonacci gap-label toy models, the candidate labels lie in $\mathbb{Z}[\phi]$ (the ring of integers of $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$), subject to the operator and trace-image hypotheses. This constrains an algebraic search space; it does not prove that observed masses are gap labels or that the labels are stable topological defects.*

*Proof sketch.* The icosahedral projection maps $\mathbb{Z}^6$ to coordinates in $N_\parallel\,\mathbb{Z}[\phi]$ (that is, $\mathbb{Z}[\phi]$ up to the row normalization of V2 Ch02 §2.1.1), and the closed 1D Fibonacci toy model has gap labels in the corresponding dimension group (cf. Appendix DPS §DPS.4). Lifting that result to a physical 6D AKN operator and trace image remains open. The Galois group of $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})/\mathbb{Q}$ is $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, with the non-trivial automorphism $\phi\mapsto-\phi^{-1}$. This is an algebraic coordinate transformation; it does **not** fix the sign of a physical susceptibility, self-energy, screening, or anti-screening response.

Integer membership is neither necessary nor sufficient for physical topological stability. Stability requires a declared order-parameter target, index map, admissible boundary/cell interpolation, an action with a metastable representative, and preservation under the dynamics. Conversely, a non-integer exponent does not imply rapid decay. The values $N=0,11,17$ in $m=m_e\phi^N$ are candidate/postdictive labels, with $11$ and $17$ remaining Tier 3 anchors pending O.5/O.14/O.15; the quark-sector irrational exponents (V3 Ch10) and the irrational exponents in $s_{23}$, $s_{13}$ (Open Problem O.5) remain ansätze. Thus FTH.5 closes only the conditional algebraic candidate space, not a particle spectrum. $\square$
