## PRG.3 Epistemic Governance

### Hard Falsification (per-row gates)
**Hard Falsification — per-row gates:**

The registered core-architecture branch is rejected if ANY of the following per-row tolerances is exceeded by $\geq 1$ independent precision measurement. These tolerances were set after the corresponding measurements were known, so they act as postdiction-consistency tolerances: no future measurement can move $m_\mu/m_e$ (known to 22 ppb) or $\alpha$ across them, and only the tau gate can still change outcome. As evidence that φ or icosahedral structure is special, the φ-formula matches these gates check are **Tier 4**: the preregistered constant control test (v3, NEGATIVE-FRAGILE; App PSC §PSC.9.6) found φ's pattern of matches on 21 constants not distinguishable from that of random constants (58 of 300 random bases did at least as well). The test "excludes, at power ≥ 0.8, an effect in which φ hits at least 6 of the 8 informative rows through a real effect; weaker effects are not excluded." The numbers are kept as descriptive arithmetic.

- Muon mass $m_\mu$ outside 100 ppm of $m_e \cdot \phi^{11} \cdot (1 + 5\alpha + \phi^8\alpha^2)$ (registry P.1; `verify_muon_mass.py`).
- Bare fine-structure prediction $\alpha^{-1}=360\phi^{-2}$ outside 5000 ppm of the independent CODATA 2022 target $\alpha^{-1}=137.035999177$ (Mohr et al. 2025, DOI 10.1103/RevModPhys.97.025002; registry P.13b). The current 3442.6 ppm comparison is inside this broad band but remains **OPEN-CONDITIONAL**, not a precision validation; reproducing $360\phi^{-2}$ is a separate tight internal implementation regression. The formula is a Tier 3 numerical relation (Tier 3 600-cell multiplier; mechanism open — the RT solid-angle construction is withdrawn, V1 §13.2.1–§13.2.2), and the residual remains Tier 3 pending O.19/O.5; see App ULA §ULA.7.1.
- Bilayer-corrected $\alpha^{-1}$ outside 100 ppm of the corrected formula (registry P.1d).
- Tau mass $m_\tau$ outside 55 ppm of $m_e \cdot \phi^{17} \cdot (1 - 3.6\alpha)$ (registry P.1b; tri-state: 0-50 PASS, 50-55 AT-GATE TENSION, $\geq55$ FAIL). The 55 ppm edge was introduced in v1.2.0; whether it predates knowledge of the ~50 ppm residual is not documented.

There is no single aggregate "precision window" — each gate is row-specific. The proton row P.2 is not in this list: its 500 ppm band around $m_e \cdot \phi^{15 + \phi^{-1}}$ is a postdiction band, not a live falsifier, because $m_p$ is already measured to $\sim10^{-10}$ and the $+\phi^{-1}$ sheet handle is offset-fitted (registry P.2; `verify_proton_mass.py`).

The electron row P.1c is not in this core-architecture list, but its own pre-commitment is binding: the pre-committed 1000 ppm rule for $m_e = M_P\phi^{-107}(1-5\alpha)$ is missed at 1006 ppm, and App FSM reports the row as a FAIL of that rule. The later 1500 ppm band is not applied retroactively. The top-quark row P.24 is not in the list either, and the same standard applies to it: its written PDG $\pm5\sigma$ rule (registered since v1.2.0) is missed at 5.18σ, so App FSM reports it as a FAIL of that rule; the residual is a Tier 3 state-level residual pending O.5. The bottom-quark row P.23 is judged by the same standard: $m_b=4134.69$ MeV lies $6.9\sigma$ below PDG 2024 $m_b(m_b)=4183\pm7$ MeV, so it is reported as a failure. Its registered window 4130–4230 MeV was centred on the measurement rather than on the prediction, so it could not test the prediction and is withdrawn as a test; the residual is a Tier 3 state-level residual pending O.5, and the mass-scheme question is open.
