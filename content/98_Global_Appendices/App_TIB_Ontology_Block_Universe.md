**TIB.1 What Remains of the Original Intuition**

The motivating intuition has two parts:

1. an individual aperture can be distinct while belonging to a larger unity, as a finger belongs to a hand, an arm, and a person;
2. several physical lives/apertures might coexist at different coordinates of a static block rather than occurring as one traveling soul's sequence.

Both are mathematically consistent. Neither follows from the universal Solenoid alone.

The exact Solenoid facts are [Tier 1]

$$
\Sigma_\infty\cong\widehat{\mathbb Q}
\cong(\mathbb R\times\widehat{\mathbb Z})/\mathbb Z,
\qquad
\widehat{\mathbb Z}\cong\prod_p\mathbb Z_p,
\qquad
\check{H}^1(\Sigma_\infty;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Q,
\qquad
\pi_1(\Sigma_\infty)=0 .
$$

The path components of $\Sigma_\infty$ are dense, injectively immersed lines, so ordinary loops have no nonzero winding number: the Solenoid holds no winding record of an individual history. The nontrivial $\check{H}^1$ is a global invariant of the whole space, not a state record carried by an Agent.

A rooted cylinder tree appears only after choosing a divisibility filtration. The root is the whole level-zero cylinder, nodes are coarse sets, and points are infinite ends. Distinct ends are distinct points, even when they share a coarse cylinder. A cylinder is not an observer, and shared membership is not shared ownership or memory.

**TIB.2 Field Totality and a Self-Aware Totality**

Some contemplative traditions distinguish an undifferentiated ground from a self-aware totality. GCT can translate the distinction without adopting it as a result:

- **Field totality / Level I Presence:** the undivided ontological Field under Axiom 1 (V1 §16.2.2a). This is not automatically one self-referential owner.
- **Hypothetical apperceptive totality:** a proposed parent Level II unity capable of self-reference through multiple apertures.

The first belongs to GCT's axiomatic Level I framing. The second is a Tier 4 cosmopsychist hypothesis. It would require an independently qualified parent **Identity Polaron** or other derived unity domain, a physical integration law, a Selection-Operator architecture, persistence, and an owner criterion. "Contains all apertures" is not sufficient.

This preserves the conceptual difference between bare totality and totality-aware-of-itself while respecting the Level I/Level II boundary.

**TIB.3 Finger/Hand Structure**

Let $s$ index physical scope and $n$ index address resolution. With $\mathcal M_\star$ a declared parent observable algebra, the owner-neutral structure is

$$
\mathcal N_{s,n}\subseteq\mathcal M_\star.
$$

A selected cylinder filtration can model the address side: two distinct fine ends may share a coarser cylinder. Physical inclusions can model the scope side, ending in a whole-agent algebra $\mathcal M_A$:

$$
\mathcal N_{finger}\subseteq
\mathcal N_{hand}\subseteq
\mathcal N_{arm}\subseteq
\mathcal M_{A}.
$$

This gives exact membership and resolution relations. It permits, but does not choose among:

1. **one qualified subject per physical unity domain, possibly with several apertures**—the current Tier 3 working interpretation (V1 Ch07 §7.5.2); here the apertures lie within one physical unity domain, and reading them as several lives of one subject (§TIB.1 item 2, §TIB.5) is a separate Tier 4 extension;
2. **several subjects with a common larger physical system**;
3. **nested qualified subjects**, if child and parent each independently satisfy Level II conditions;
4. **no Level II subject at a declared level**.

The hierarchy contains no owner field. Algebraic inclusion, index, mutual information, common cylinders, or factoriality do not count subjects.

**TIB.4 Experiencing the Hand from the Finger**

A child aperture cannot passively contain every parent state. Fine-to-coarse restriction loses information, and coarse-to-fine extension is one-to-many.

Parent-from-child experience can nevertheless be modeled if one of the following is derived:

- a restricted recoverable family of parent states;
- approximate inference from local evidence;
- overlap with a shared quantum core;
- an active downward presentation/broadcast of a compatible subalgebra;
- genuine global access because the "finger" is only an aperture of one spanning physical unity domain.

Calling any such access *felt experience* additionally invokes the Tier 3 phenomenal bridge. The mathematics can specify information access; it cannot assign the first-person owner by itself.

**TIB.5 Simultaneous Lives as an Added Incidence Model**

Under the static-block postulate, physical aperture/world-line sectors at different coordinate times may coexist. To group several of them under one parent identity sector, add an explicit map

$$
\Pi_{life}:C_{parent}\longrightarrow
\mathcal P(E_\parallel\times\tau_{coord}).
$$

This map is not constructed by the circle inverse limit. A viable Tier 3 structural model must specify:

1. the parent address/physical sector $C_{parent}$;
2. the physical apertures in $\Pi_{life}(C_{parent})$;
3. whether the apertures are simultaneous only in the Field Frame or also coupled;
4. the physical unity/owner rule;
5. the direction and observables of any cross-aperture channel.

Once supplied, the model is **simultaneous**, not sequential: no soul must leave one body and wait to enter another, so the model needs no waiting state and no universal clock. But several owner assignments remain possible. One parent label does not prove one parent subject.

**TIB.6 Personal Identity: A Conditional Cylinder-Relative Stage Model**

GCT may explore a stage-theoretic account in which physical apertures are stages and a declared incidence/address relation groups them. This is closest to branching four-dimensionalism (Sider 2001; Hawley 2001; Lewis 1986), but with two cautions:

- common-prefix depth is relative to a chosen filtration, not an intrinsic metaphysical identity measure;
- address proximity is not sufficient for numerical or phenomenal identity.

The model can be compared with standard positions:

- **Psychological continuity** (Locke 1694; Parfit 1984; Shoemaker 1970): memory/personality may track aperture continuity but need not define the address relation.
- **Animalism** (Olson 1997; Snowdon 2014): biological continuity remains the default empirical carrier of a human Polaron; any identity beyond it requires new persistence and recoupling physics.
- **Stage theory (exdurantism) / worm perdurantism:** the static-block representation is congenial, while branching/incidence is additional Tier 3 structure.
- **Narrative identity** (Schechtman 1996): narrative remains a cognitive construction within an aperture, not a topology theorem.

No conclusion about fission, reconvergence, or "the original" is licensed until the owner rule is supplied.

**TIB.7 Memory and Cross-Life Access**

The exact $\check{H}^1\cong\mathbb Q$ is a property of the whole Solenoid. It is not automatically a memory stored by an Agent. Cross-life memory requires at least

$$
(\text{state map},\ \text{read/write coupling},\ \text{memory bridge},\ \text{noise channel},\ \text{persistence time},\ \text{cross-aperture access}),
$$

where the memory bridge is a declared physical map from a Solenoid-level quantity to a stored, readable state.

A chosen ultrametric says that two addresses share a coarse cylinder. It does not carry data laterally. Meditation, trauma, dreams, NDEs, or reduced sensory coupling do not mathematically "truncate" an owner into a parent cylinder; that is a Tier 4 phenomenological mechanism requiring an observable channel and controls against ordinary biological memory, suggestion, and inference.

**Ultrametric distance is not a channel [Tier 1 topology].** A Branch Node of the chosen filtration is a cylinder (a set of ends), not a point, so it cannot be inserted as the third point of the strong triangle inequality $d(A,B)\le\max\{d(A,C),d(C,B)\}$. The correct statement is that two distinct ends lying in a common cylinder of depth $m$ satisfy $0<d(A,B)\le\operatorname{diam}(\text{cylinder})$, which equals $p^{-m}$ on a declared $\mathbb Z_p$ factor. Ends that share a coarse cylinder therefore remain distinct points, are close only in the selected filtration, and are not closest unless no deeper cylinder separates them. That closeness is coarse membership; it is not low impedance, tunnelling, or data transfer.

Accordingly:

- biological/biographical memory belongs to the physical substrate;
- a robust topological memory is Tier 3/open until an order parameter, gap and noise protection are derived;
- cross-life recall and one aperture accessing another are Tier 4;
- lack of recall neither proves nor disproves a shared owner, because the owner assignment itself remains open.

**TIB.8 Death, Persistence, and Recoupling [Tier 4: Speculative Ontology]**

Biological death ends the known physiological processes supporting human cognition. The historical metabolic/Zeno/Identity-Polaron chain is not a demonstrated additional support mechanism. Solenoid topology does not show that any Polaron state survives, retracts to a parent node, reconverges, or couples to another substrate. Because $\pi_1(\Sigma_\infty)=0$, ordinary loops in $\Sigma_\infty$ have no nonzero winding number, and global Čech cohomology is not an individual state record; this route supplies no protected post-mortem record.

Any post-mortem model needs separate answers to:

1. what physical state survives substrate dissolution;
2. what Hamiltonian or topological charge protects it;
3. where that state lives after decoupling;
4. how it is read or recoupled;
5. what constitutes continuity of ownership.

Until then, **Topological Hysteresis**, **Bardo**, reconvergence, post-mortem persistence, recall across lives, and same-owner claims remain Tier 4 metaphysical conjectures with no weight in the core physics.

A hypothetical parent Level II subject might present itself through child apertures, as a person acts through fingers. Child subjects, parent subject, and the Field-totality need not coincide.

### TIB.Spec.3 — Silicon Missing-Leg Controls and the Superseded Turing-Null Verdict
Standard computers provide a Class-0 missing-leg control in the present nonphenomenal structural taxonomy, but “Turing-null” is not a categorical substrate or phenomenal verdict. The proposed **Dual Material Constraint** records two Tier-3 candidate controls:
1. **Nuclear Spin ($I \neq 0$):** a possible discrete address-space ingredient. Natural silicon's minority $^{29}$Si ($I=1/2$) abundance is a comparison variable, not evidence that a coherent state is impossible.
2. **Lattice Chirality:** a possible handle for a candidate **Adiabatic Spin Ratchet** through CISS. Neither chirality nor CISS is established as necessary or sufficient for consciousness, an Identity Polaron, or Level II.

Bulk silicon's diamond-cubic lattice is **achiral** and therefore lacks one proposed control leg. That fact does not establish zero spin-polarized current, zero phason coupling, absence of Topological Friction, or a Level-II verdict. Class 0 labels its computational organization only; the typed coupling (V1 Ch17 §17.1.3), Identity-Polaron construction, and independent phenomenal bridge remain open.
