### **Appendix SNE: Selection, No-Signalling, and Energy**

## SNE.1 The Closed System Identity

Geometric Consciousness Theory (GCT) maintains that the universe is a closed system at the fundamental level of the Bulk. Under the Tier 3 Wheeler-DeWitt structural postulate (V1 Ch05), the universal wavefunction $|\Psi\rangle$ satisfies the constraint:
$$ \hat{H} |\Psi\rangle = 0 $$

For the following formal consistency check, $\mathcal{F}_{sel}$ is stipulated as an Agent-frame conditionalization map rather than a modification of the global Hamiltonian $\hat{H}$. This is a model definition, not a derivation or physical realization of a Selection Operator.

### Conditional No-Added-Energy Identity
If an observer conditions a description on branch $|\phi_k\rangle$ while the stipulated global state remains the unitary superposition
$$ |\Psi\rangle = \sum_i c_i |\phi_i\rangle $$
then the unchanged global Wheeler-DeWitt expectation remains
$$ \langle \Psi | \hat{H} | \Psi \rangle = 0 $$
This identity says only that descriptive conditioning does not alter the stipulated global state. It does not prove that a physical Agent collapses, redistributes, or steers amplitudes, nor that a non-unitary $\mathcal F_{sel}$ can be implemented without an explicit open-system energy ledger.

## SNE.2 The Metabolic Trade

This section records a **hypothetical energy ledger** for a future intent-to-metabolism bridge. No realized physical $\mathcal F_{sel}$, mental force, or material-to-vacuum transducer is presently derived; the bookkeeping is conditional on supplying such a bridge independently.

### The Cost of Thought
Within that conditional surrogate, define **Selection Work** ($W_{sel}$) as a placeholder for energy transferred through a specified material action channel toward a declared target configuration. The First-Law ledger would read:
$$ \Delta E_{bio} = W_{sel} + \Delta Q_{heat} $$
This identity enforces energy accounting only after the terms are physically defined. It does not establish a "Mental Force," an exact topological torque, or an ATP-powered Zeno interface (the microtubule-lumen interface remains Tier 3 candidate biochemistry, App ORQ O.34). ATP hydrolysis is a measured metabolic energy source and therefore a possible budget ceiling, not evidence for phason bias or vacuum winding.

**Conditional surrogate ledger:**
1. **Input candidate:** Measured chemical potential ($\Delta \mu_{ATP}$).
2. **Transduction placeholder:** A hypothetical Zeno/THz material channel whose coupling, efficiency, and directionality remain unmeasured and underived.
3. **Target variable:** A declared GCT configuration change; neither topological winding of the vacuum lattice nor its identification with $\mathcal{F}_{sel}$ has been physically realized.

If such a bridge were established, conservation would require it to redirect supplied metabolic free energy rather than create energy. This is a constraint on a future mechanism, not evidence that an Agent presently gates metabolic entropy.

## SNE.4 Conditional Subluminal Readout for a Declared Phason Source [Tier 1 selection rule; Tier 3 subluminality premise]

**Scope of the result.** The calculation below is a conditional field-propagation statement: if a declared source enters the phason equation and couples into the physical phonon sector through the allowed strain bilinear, the physical disturbance is carried by the phonon propagator at no more than the phonon speed; it is subluminal only on the additional premise $v_{\rm phonon}\leq c$ (Step 2), which GCT's own speed hierarchy does not supply. This is distinct from the standard quantum-information no-signalling theorem, which concerns completely-positive trace-preserving (CPTP) maps, local instruments and outcome averaging on a joint state. No established GCT result presently derives a nonlocal topological correlation, entangled state, common source, or cross-aperture channel from Solenoid topology. The theorem therefore constrains a source model **after** such physical data are supplied; it does not validate those data.

**Theorem SNE.1 (Phonon-speed-bounded physical readout of a declared address coupling).** Choose an address filtration and suppose a separately derived physical source/coupling associates two apertures with a common cylinder at depth $m$. Any observable disturbance transmitted from the phason source into $E_\parallel$ through the strain-level $K_3$ phason–phonon channel propagates no faster than the physical phonon characteristic speed $v_{\rm phonon}$; it propagates no faster than $c$ only under the additional premise $v_{\rm phonon}\leq c$ [Tier 3 premise; it fails under the P3b speed map, Step 2]. Shared cylinder membership alone does not establish a correlation, common state, or access channel.

**Proof.** The proof has three steps.

**Step 1 — Selection-rule structure of the phason–phonon coupling [Tier 1 character calculation given the representations; Tier 2 GCT field assignment].** The phonon displacement $u_\parallel$ transforms as the vector representation $T_1$ of the icosahedral group $I$ (order 60), and the phason displacement $u_\perp$ transforms as the Galois-conjugate representation $T_2$ (related to $T_1$ by the $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$ automorphism $\phi \mapsto 1 - \phi$) — this is the standard quasicrystal-theory identification (Senechal 1995 *Quasicrystals and Geometry* §2.5; Janssen et al. 2018 *Aperiodic Crystals* §6.3). $T_1$ and $T_2$ are inequivalent 3-dimensional irreducible representations of $I$.

Two distinct bilinears must be examined to see which mode-couplings are allowed by $H_3$:

(i) *Displacement-level bilinear* $u_\parallel^i u_\perp^j$ (a candidate mass-like mixing term, transforming in $T_1 \otimes T_2$). With the conjugacy classes ordered $(e, 5A, 5B, 3A, 2A)$ (sizes $1, 12, 12, 20, 15$), the character of $T_1 \otimes T_2$ is the pointwise product $(9, -1, -1, 0, 1)$, and the multiplicity of the trivial irrep is

$$n_A(T_1 \otimes T_2) = \frac{1}{60}(9 - 12 - 12 + 0 + 15) = 0.$$

A direct $H_3$-invariant displacement–displacement coupling is therefore *forbidden*: no mass-like phonon–phason mixing exists at the algebraic level.

(ii) *Strain-level bilinear* $C^{mix}_{ijkl}\,\varepsilon_{ij}\,w_{kl}$. The physical phonon strain is symmetric in its two indices, $\varepsilon_{ij} = \tfrac{1}{2}(\partial_i u_\parallel^j + \partial_j u_\parallel^i)$, and therefore lives in $\mathrm{Sym}^2(T_1) = A \oplus H$ (the antisymmetric part $\Lambda^2(T_1) = T_1$ is the rotation of the displacement field, which couples through gauge-rotation invariance separately). The phason gradient $w_{kl} = \partial_k u_\perp^l$ lives in $T_1 \otimes T_2 = G \oplus H$. The strain-level mixing coupling therefore lives in
$$\mathrm{Sym}^2(T_1) \otimes (T_1 \otimes T_2) = (A \oplus H) \otimes (G \oplus H).$$
By the canonical multiplicity formula $n_A(R \otimes S) = \sum_X m_R(X)\,m_S(X)$, the multiplicity of the trivial irrep in this product equals the number of irreps appearing in both $\mathrm{Sym}^2(T_1)$ and $T_1 \otimes T_2$:
$$n_A\bigl(\mathrm{Sym}^2(T_1) \otimes (T_1 \otimes T_2)\bigr) = \sum_{X \in \{A, T_1, T_2, G, H\}} m_{\mathrm{Sym}^2(T_1)}(X)\,m_{T_1 \otimes T_2}(X).$$
Both decompositions contain $H$ exactly once; no other irrep is shared between $\mathrm{Sym}^2(T_1) = A \oplus H$ and $T_1 \otimes T_2 = G \oplus H$. Therefore $n_A = 1 \cdot 1 + 0 = 1$, and exactly one $H_3$-invariant strain–strain coupling exists, arising from the shared $H$ representation. This is the standard Socolar–Lubensky–Steinhardt / Lubensky–Ramaswamy–Toner $K_3 \varepsilon_{ij} w_{kl}$ term of icosahedral quasicrystal elasticity (Socolar et al. 1986; Lubensky et al. 1985); it is the same coupling that appears as the $C^{mix}_{ijkl}$ term of the hydrodynamic free energy in V2 Ch05 §5.2.2.

The first selection rule forbids displacement-level mixing in the declared representation assignment; a future $\mathcal F_{sel}$ source model would have to respect it, but no physical Selection Operator is derived. The second permits one ordinary strain-level quasicrystal-elasticity coupling. A separately supplied phason source could drive the phonon equation through that channel; GCT has not derived such a source or linked it to selection. **Field-typing consistency note [Tier 1 representation theory].** The same selection rule underlies the field-typing analysis of V1 Ch17 §17.1.3. Vector phason strain lies in $T_1 \otimes T_2 = G \oplus H$, while the Levi-Civita cross product $\mathbf{k} \times \boldsymbol{\sigma}$ lies in $T_1$; they share no irrep, so the cross-product coupling of V1 Eq. (17.3) gives no icosahedral scalar for the vector phason. The full tensor $\mathbf{k} \otimes \boldsymbol{\sigma} = A \oplus T_1 \oplus H$ shares exactly one $H$ with the phason strain; under full $I_h$ parity the bare contraction is a pseudoscalar, and multiplication by a time-even molecular-handedness pseudoscalar $\chi \in A_u$ permits one candidate term. This establishes only that such a channel is allowed and unique up to its coefficient and normalization. It does not insert the term into the action, fix its coefficient, or turn the conditional source of Theorem SNE.1 into a physical Dual-Material or Selection source.

The linearised equations of motion in the presence of the $K_3$ coupling read schematically
$$\rho \ddot{u}_\parallel = K_\parallel \nabla^2 u_\parallel + K_3\, \nabla\!\cdot w, \qquad \rho_{eff} \ddot{u}_\perp = K_\perp \nabla^2 u_\perp + K_3\, \nabla\!\cdot \varepsilon,$$
i.e. a $w$-source in the phason equation feeds the phonon equation through the $K_3 \nabla\!\cdot w$ term. The phonon propagator then carries that disturbance into $E_\parallel$. Step 2 controls the propagation speed.

**Step 2 — The induced signal in $E_\parallel$ is bounded by the phonon speed; bounding it by $c$ is an extra premise [Tier 2 propagation bound within the declared linear model; Tier 3 subluminality premise].** The $K_3$-induced phonon disturbance propagates as a solution of the phonon wave operator $\rho \partial_t^2 - K_\parallel \nabla^2$, whose characteristic speed is the phonon sound speed $v_{\rm phonon}$; within the declared linear model the disturbance therefore travels no faster than $v_{\rm phonon}$, regardless of the algebraic structure of the source term. The further bound $v_{\rm phonon}\leq c$ is **not derived here**. It is an additional premise: that the long-wavelength effective theory of the phonon sector is Lorentz invariant with limiting speed $c$ (cf. Volovik, G. E. (2003), *The Universe in a Helium Droplet*, Oxford University Press). That premise is in tension with GCT's own speed hierarchy: the book identifies $c$ with the phason ("second sound") speed (V2 Ch06 Postulate 6.1; V2 Ch08; Parameter Ledger row $c$), and under the Tier 3 stiffness-map hypothesis P3b the reference phonon speed is $v_\parallel\approx c_P=\phi^{9}c>c$ (App TPS §TPS.1), with the phason sector the "soft" carrier relative to the "hard" phonon bonds (App ULA §ULA.3). **Under P3b the premise fails**, and Step 2 then bounds the readout only by $v_{\rm phonon}$, which is not a subluminality result. The subluminality of the readout is therefore conditional on the extra premise $v_{\rm phonon}\leq c$ [Tier 3], not a load-bearing physical fact of the GCT model.

**Step 3 — Address proximity is not physical proximity or a correlation law.** Two distinct Solenoid ends may share a cylinder in a chosen filtration while remaining different points. The Realization/Incidence map to physical locations $\mathbf{r}_{\parallel}^{(i)}$ and $\mathbf{r}_{\parallel}^{(j)}$ is additional Tier 3 data, as is any common-source or cross-aperture correlation kernel. The topology does not put the apertures at one Solenoid point and does not remove the need to specify how a measurable correlation is produced.

**Conclusion.** The field-theoretic calculation establishes a conditional propagation bound: if a declared phason source drives an $E_\parallel$ observable through the $K_3$ channel, the readout propagates no faster than the phonon speed, and subluminally only on the extra premise $v_{\rm phonon}\leq c$ (which fails under P3b). It does **not** derive simultaneous access, static co-location, Bell correlations, or a cross-aperture channel from shared cylinders. Those premises require an independent physical law and must also satisfy the standard CPTP no-signalling constraints. $\square$

**Scope corollary.** Bell-inequality violations cannot presently be recast as Solenoid-cylinder correlations by this theorem. Such a model would need an explicit entangled state, local instruments, outcome statistics reproducing the quantum correlations, and a proof of no signalling after outcome averaging. The present $K_3$ calculation constrains only the physical field-readout channel [Tier 1 selection rule; Tier 3 subluminality premise; Solenoid/Bell identification Tier 3/open].

**Consequence under P3b [Tier 3; open problem App ORQ O.48(b)].** If the Tier 3 stiffness-map hypothesis P3b holds, the phonon speed is $v_{\rm phonon}\approx c_P=\phi^{9}c>c$ (Step 2), so a declared phason source driving an $E_\parallel$ observable through the $K_3$ channel would be a superluminal channel inside the model. This tension with relativistic causality is not resolved here; it is registered as App ORQ O.48(b).

## SNE.5 Born-Marginal Constraint on Biased Selection [Tier 1 — standard QI consequence; its application to GCT selection is the open problem App ORQ O.48(a)]

The standard no-signalling identity used in V1 Ch09 §9.4.3 sums unnormalised projections, $\sum_k \mathrm{Tr}_A[(\hat P_k\otimes\hat I_B)\rho(\hat P_k\otimes\hat I_B)]=\rho_B$, so it holds when the outcomes at A occur with the Born weights $p_k=\mathrm{Tr}[(\hat P_k\otimes\hat I_B)\rho]$. If a will-, resonance- or impedance-biased Selection Operator reweighted A's outcomes to $q_k\neq p_k$, B's outcome-averaged state would be $\sum_k q_k\,\rho_{B|k}\neq\rho_B$ in general, and the bias would signal superluminally (Gisin 1990; Polchinski 1991). Any selection bias that GCT entertains (V1 Ch09 §9.5.3; V1 Ch10 §10.3.2) must therefore leave the outcome-averaged marginals at their Born values on every subsystem. Local unitary or CPTP steering before an outcome is registered meets this constraint, because it changes the state to which the Born rule applies rather than the rule itself. No mechanism that biases individual outcomes while meeting the constraint is stated in the manuscript; this gap is App ORQ O.48(a).
