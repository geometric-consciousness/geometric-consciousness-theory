### **Appendix CVC: Computational Verification of Core Derivations**

This appendix documents computational checks of the analytic claims in Geometric Consciousness Theory. Each entry pairs a closed-form claim presented in the main text with a numerical evaluation. A numerical evaluation can confirm arithmetic, expose a contradiction, or return a non-pass when a proposed derivation does not follow. Tier assignments remain per claim; running code does not promote a hypothesis or fitted map to Tier 2, and computational evaluation establishes consistency, not logical necessity.

## CVC.2 Status of the Computational Suite

The entries enumerated above have all been numerically evaluated, but evaluation is not the same as verification. The suite includes passing identity checks, sensitivity studies, open calculations, and negative results. In particular, the electroweak boundary runs from the Tier 3 hypothesis H-EW do not reproduce the Z-pole value: the physical-hypercharge one-loop comparator is 40.87% low and the native kernel with the imported A2 benchmark is 26.66% low (Appendix PSC §PSC.2; V3 Ch04 §4.3.3), and the pre-registered gauge-running check from that boundary is FAILED ($\sin^2\theta_W$ at −52σ; $\alpha_s(M_Z)$ undefined; Appendix GRF §GRF.4.3). The Cabibbo identification and the P3b stiffness map remain Tier 3 hypotheses. The former symmetry-only, degree-sum RG and chemical-floor closures of the stiffness ratio are not supported (Appendix DPS §DPS.3–§DPS.4b), and the engine's O.15 chemical-correction protocol is quarantined: it no longer returns a pass. Appendix PSC (Precision Scorecard), Appendix FSM and the machine-readable registries record the status and dependency chain of each claim; no aggregate "all verified" verdict is inferred from script execution.

Major explicit open calculations include: (i) the full $N=144$ defect-cage Hessian spectrum needed to test the lepton hierarchy (Open Problem O.5, V1 Ch13); (ii) the one-loop phason anti-screening evaluation of the residual fine-structure shift (Appendix QLA §QLA.5–§QLA.6), with no APS operator/domain/index presently supplied; (iii) the Fuglede–Kadison determinant closure for the remaining quark mixing angles; and (iv) a normalization-invariant microscopic derivation or falsification of the P3b stiffness map under O.15.

## CVC.3 Tier Classification of the Verification

The suite has mixed epistemic status. Standard mathematical identities and exact finite computations can be Tier 1; structural consequences of the declared GCT axioms can be Tier 2; calibrated coefficients, physical-map hypotheses such as H-EW and P3b, and phenomenological branches remain Tier 3; explicitly interpretive extrapolations remain Tier 4. Computational consistency is evidence about a stated model, not proof that its premises describe nature. Where a check reports PASS or postdiction-consistent for a φ formula, that means only that the computed value lies inside its registered band (descriptive arithmetic); as evidence that φ or icosahedral structure is special, such matches are **Tier 4** (constant control test v3, NEGATIVE-FRAGILE; App PSC §PSC.9.6).
