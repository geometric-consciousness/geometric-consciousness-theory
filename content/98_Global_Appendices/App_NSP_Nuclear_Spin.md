### **Appendix NSP: Nuclear Spin and P-adic Levels**

Geometric Consciousness Theory (GCT) proposes nuclear-spin systems as **candidate physical registers** in the biophysical interface [Tier 3]. Nuclear-spin Hilbert spaces are not intrinsically $p$-adic, are not themselves Solenoid addresses, and do not establish a definitive Mind–Brain Bridge. Mapping a physical register to a Solenoid address, or treating it as memory, would require separately derived coupling, read/write, noise, protection and lifetime dynamics. Any $p$-primary hierarchy used below is therefore a chosen coding/filtration model, not an isomorphism forced by nuclear spin or by subjective time. Every measurement discussed here tests an ordinary material proxy; no phason, Selection-Operator or Level-II inference follows from it (V1 Ch17 §17.1.3).

## NSP.1 The Candidate Spin Register
GCT considers stable or driven nuclear spins as candidate **ordinary material registers**. The $I = 1/2$, $I = 1$, and $I = 5/2$ manifolds of selected biogenic isotopes supply finite quantum state spaces (the $I = 1$ register covers the dominant natural-abundance $^{14}$N, present in every Trp indole sidechain at 99.64% abundance; integer-spin registers are considered alongside half-integer ones). Earlier editions called these states "Ports" for the conscious observer; that is unsupported mechanism language and is withdrawn. The substrate mechanisms are isotope-specific: $^{31}$P nuclear spins ($I=1/2$; no quadrupole moment) are protected, in the Fisher pathway, by the Posner-molecule $\mathrm{Ca}_9(\mathrm{PO}_4)_6$ coordination network; Trp indole $^{14}$N/$^1$H spins enter ordinary radical-pair hyperfine dynamics (reviewed in Hore-Mouritsen 2016) and possible CISS measurements (§NSP.5; CISS is not part of the Hore-Mouritsen radical-pair review). Fisher's 2015 proposal assigns long coherence to phosphate/pyrophosphate nuclear-spin transport and an idealized Posner-molecule channel (with originally much longer isolated-cluster estimates), while later analyses such as Player-Hore substantially reduce the physiological expectation. The historical Trp target $T_2 > 1\,\mu$s (V1 Ch17 §17.1.2 CAUTION block) and the historical drive frequency are imported material-proxy conditions, not a Zeno-Drive operating budget, a Selection clock, or a consciousness requirement (V1 Ch17 §17.1.3). Posner coherence numbers are secondary material context only.

## NSP.2 Chosen Address Depth and Neural Frequency [Tier 3 mapping]
The brain's neural oscillation hierarchy ($\delta$, $\theta$, $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$) is treated here as a Tier 3 candidate mapping onto levels of a **chosen** $p$-primary address filtration. ## NSP.5 Chirality and the CISS Coupling Channel — The Second Pillar of the Dual Material Constraint

The Dual Material Constraint of V1 Ch16 §16.2.6 proposes two candidate control properties for Level-II substrate tests: (a) non-zero nuclear spin ($I \neq 0$) and (b) **molecular chirality** with CISS. These are empirically useful missing-leg contrasts, not established necessary or sufficient conditions for Level II; the typed phason interaction and independent phenomenal bridge remain open.

**NSP.5.1 The CISS effect — empirical foundations.** Electron transport through chiral molecular structures exhibits a robust spin-polarisation across the wider $\sim 5$–$60\%$ range when protein-system data is included alongside the original DNA-substrate measurements: the DNA-equivalent upper edge $\sim 60\%$ comes from Göhler et al. 2011 *Science* 331:894 for double-stranded DNA self-assembled monolayers; protein-system measurements typically sit lower in the band — Mishra et al. 2013 *PNAS* 110:14872 reports $\sim 14\%$ at room temperature for bacteriorhodopsin, and α-helical oligopeptide measurements (Aragonès et al. 2017 *Small* 13:1602519; Tassinari et al. 2018 *Adv. Mater.* 30:1706423) span $\sim 5$–$15\%$; Kettner et al. 2018 *J. Phys. Chem. Lett.* 9:2025 measures helicene small-molecule monolayers as a complementary chiral-system class. The wider $[5, 60]\%$ range therefore brackets *both* the DNA-equivalent ordered-helix upper edge and the protein-system lower edge; the narrower $[10, 60]\%$ band cited in some surveys (Naaman, Paltiel & Waldeck 2019 *Nat. Rev. Chem.* 3:250) reflects a DNA-weighted lower cut. The framework's missing-leg control classes (silicon, achiral diamond-cubic, etc.) are unchanged under either reading; neither reading yields a Level-II exclusion verdict. The polarisation direction is determined by the helix handedness: D-helices polarise one spin orientation along the transport axis, L-helices the opposite. Critically, CISS persists in the absence of an external magnetic field — the helical geometry itself acts as the symmetry-breaking element coupling charge transport to spin. The underlying mechanism is debated (spin-orbit coupling enhanced by helical confinement; chiral-induced effective magnetic field from electron-vibration coupling; details in Naaman-Paltiel-Waldeck 2019 §3), but the empirical effect is robust and reproducible across DNA, proteins, and small chiral molecules.

**NSP.5.2 Why GCT tests chirality + nuclear spin jointly.** The proposed Identity-Polaron programme varies an addressable nuclear-spin register and a chirality/CISS handle as independent candidate controls. Their conjunction is a Tier-3 physical ansatz, not a derived phason coupling or a necessary/sufficient Level-II criterion.
