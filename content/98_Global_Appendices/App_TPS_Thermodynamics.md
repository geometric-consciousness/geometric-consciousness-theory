### **Appendix TPS: Thermodynamics of Perpendicular Space**

**TPS.1 Phason Density of States**

To resolve the thermal catastrophe of the vacuum crystallization (Chapter 7), we must quantify the thermodynamic capacity of the internal manifold $E_\perp$. We analyze the **Density of States (DOS)**, $g(\omega)$, for both the phonon (parallel) and phason (perpendicular) sectors.

The density of states in a $d$-dimensional medium with dispersion relation $\omega = v k$ scales as:
$$ g(\omega) \propto \frac{\omega^{d-1}}{v^d} $$
* **Parallel Sector ($E_\parallel$):** The model identifies these excitations as phonons and provisionally associates their reference stiffness and speed with the Planck scales, $K_\parallel \approx E_P/\ell_P^3$ and $v_\parallel \approx c_P$, the bare lattice speed of V2 Ch08 §8.1.3. In the Parameter Ledger convention (row $c$) $c_P$ is distinct from the observed speed of light $c$, which the book proposes to identify with the phason (second-sound) speed (V2 Ch06 Postulate 6.1, a declared open programme).
* **Perpendicular Sector ($E_\perp$):** The model identifies these excitations as phasons. The numerical assignments $K_\perp/K_\parallel = \phi^{-18}$ and $v_\perp/v_\parallel = \phi^{-9}$ (so $v_\perp = c \approx c_P \phi^{-9}$) are **Tier 3, conditional on the Parameter Ledger stiffness-map hypothesis P3b (O.15(b)) and a common inertial normalization**; only the integer $D = 18$ is Tier 2, and the ratio is not a projector or point-group invariant (App ULA §ULA.3).

Conditional on the linear, isotropic dispersion model and the P3b speed map, the ratio of the density of states at a given energy $E = \hbar \omega$ is
$$ \frac{g_\perp(\omega)}{g_\parallel(\omega)} \approx \left( \frac{v_\parallel}{v_\perp} \right)^3 \approx (\phi^9)^3 = \phi^{27} \approx 4.4 \times 10^5 $$
This $\phi^{27}$ factor is therefore conditional arithmetic, not an independently derived thermodynamic result. Within that branch it favors the internal space; the proposed primary thermodynamic sink is the much larger **Holographic Phase Space** of the bulk lattice [Tier 4].

**TPS.2 The Heat Capacity Ratio**

The heat capacity $C_V$ of a system is proportional to the number of accessible degrees of freedom ($N_{dof}$).
* **Physical Manifold ($N_\parallel$):** The number of degrees of freedom in the observable universe is limited by the number of nodes in the 3D projection slice.
* **Internal Manifold ($N_\perp$):** The number of degrees of freedom in the internal space corresponds to the total number of nodes in the 6D parent lattice that are causally connected within the horizon but not projected into the physical slice.

For a causal patch defined by the Hubble Horizon, the **Holographic Principle** caps the effective number of degrees of freedom at the screen count $(R_H/\ell_P)^2 \sim 10^{122}$; the bulk volume count $(R_H/\ell_P)^3 \sim 10^{183}$ would exceed that cap. Invoking a bulk reservoir much larger than the screen is therefore in tension with the same principle [Tier 4].
Within the candidate architecture, this immense heat-capacity ratio would drive the released latent heat of crystallization entropically into the bulk modes of $E_\perp$; the partition function and coupling that would make this quantitative are open (§TPS.3). The physical slice $E_\parallel$ acts as a low-capacity "surface" in thermal contact with a candidate large reservoir [Tier 4].
