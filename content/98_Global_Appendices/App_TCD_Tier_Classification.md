### **Appendix TCD: Tier Classification Decision Tree**

**TCD.1 Classification Algorithm**

To maintain the highest standards of scientific integrity, every claim within the GCT framework is subjected to a rigorous **Epistemic Filter**. This decision tree ensures that the reader can distinguish between logical certainties, geometric modeling, and phenomenological calibration.

**Exclusive Tier 0 gate:** The single first-person datum that experience is occurring, i.e. that there is presence now, is Tier 0. It is neither postulated nor derived; doubting or dreaming is itself experience. Exactly this one item belongs to Tier 0; no other claim may ever enter it. Axiom 1 remains the stipulated universal Level I generalisation at Tier 1, motivated by the datum rather than identical to it. The objective-idealist ontology remains a Tier 3 parsimony choice, and Level II substrate verdicts remain open (Tier 3) / `NOT_EVALUATED`. All other claims follow the decision logic below.

**The Decision Logic:**
1. **Does the claim follow necessarily from the Axioms (Presence and Intelligibility) and formal mathematics?**
 * **YES:** Proceed to **Tier 1**.
2. **Is the claim an architectural choice adopted for parsimony and empirical adequacy (not deductively forced by the Ontological Axioms alone), or an exact mathematical consequence that follows once such a choice is assumed?**
 * **YES:** Proceed to **Tier 1/2 (Architectural Postulate + Conditional Mathematics)**. Conditional mathematics never promotes the premise it assumes.
3. **Does the claim depend on the specific Icosahedral Cut-and-Project ansatz ($\mathbb{Z}^6 \to 3D$, organized by the conditional $E_8\to H_4\to H_3$ chain; $\mathbb{Z}^6$ is not a sublattice of $E_8$)?**
 * **YES:** Is the result a structural necessity of that specific geometry?
 * **YES:** Proceed to **Tier 2**.
4. **Does the claim require calibration to empirical data or rely on unproven hydrodynamic assumptions?**
 * **YES:** Proceed to **Tier 3**.
5. **Is the claim an exploratory calculation where the dimensional structure is correct but the numerical coefficient is uncertain by more than one order of magnitude, or an ontological extension outside the core falsifiable theory?**
 * **YES:** Proceed to **Tier 4 (Speculative / Exploratory)**. All Tier 4 numerical claims carry explicit order-of-magnitude bounds and are segregated from the core falsifiable program.
