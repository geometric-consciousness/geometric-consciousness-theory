### **The Epistemic Tier System**

In the interest of scientific rigor and epistemic discipline, we categorize the claims in this text according to a strict epistemic hierarchy. We distinguish the first-person datum from stipulated axioms, what is logically forced by the axioms, what follows robustly from the specific geometric projection ansatz, and what remains calibrated phenomenology.

![GCT separates claims by epistemic status, from the single Tier 0 first-person datum through Tier 1 axioms to Tier 4 exploratory extensions, so foundational certainty cannot leak into downstream hypotheses.](content/Figures/Frontmatter/Figure GF.1.1.svg)

* **Tier 0 (Bedrock / First-Person Datum):** Experience is occurring: there is presence now. This datum is neither a postulate nor a derived result and cannot be coherently doubted: doubting is itself an occurrence of experience, and even in a dream the experience occurs. **One-item rule: exactly this one datum belongs to Tier 0; no other claim may ever be assigned to it.** Axiom 1 generalises this datum by stipulation [Tier 1] to universal Level I intrinsic non-subjective presence in all Field configurations; it is not identical to the datum and does not inherit its certainty. The objective-idealist ontology remains a Tier 3 parsimony choice (V1 Ch06). Tier 0 supplies no Level II verdict for any substrate; those verdicts remain open (Tier 3) / `NOT_EVALUATED`.

* **Tier 1 (Structural / Axiomatic):** Foundational axioms and logical necessities that define the GCT framework. These are either **postulated** as irreducible starting assumptions, or **proven by contradiction** to be the unique consistent solution. If these axioms are true, the subsequent Tier 2 results must follow.
 * *Examples:* the canonical form of the Wheeler–DeWitt constraint $\hat{H}\Psi=0$, whose adoption as a closed-system postulate is separately Tier 3; the **Lattice Action Postulate** ($\hat{\hbar}=1$); the masslessness of the photon ($m_\gamma=0$) as an external Tier 1 Standard-Model fact, which rests on unbroken local $\mathrm{U}(1)$ gauge invariance and the Ward identity (standard field theory, not a GCT-specific derivation; GCT has not yet reproduced it, since its photon sector is a declared open programme, V2 Ch06; compactness of a phase variable alone does not force a flat potential).
 * *Note:* **Tier 1 denotes either a structural axiom or a proof of logical necessity.** These are the irreducible assumptions and forced conclusions that anchor the entire theoretical edifice.

* **Tier 1/2 (Architectural Postulate + Conditional Mathematics):** Architectural choices adopted for parsimony and empirical adequacy, together with exact mathematical consequences that follow once those choices are assumed. The architectural premise is not deductively forced by the Ontological Axioms, and it is not converted into a theorem by its downstream conditional consequences. *Rule:* conditional mathematics never promotes the premise it assumes.
 * *Examples:* The **Icosahedral Projection** ($E_8\to H_4$, followed by a rank-six $H_3$ module with adopted primitive Bravais lattice $P\cong\mathbb{Z}^6$): conditionally preferred by the registered comparison under parsimony, empirical adequacy, maximal polyhedral point symmetry, aperiodicity, the five-fold rhombic-triacontahedral window, and golden arithmetic. Rank 6 is fixed by full icosahedral indexing, but the closed Bravais classification also contains $F\cong D_6$ and $I\cong D_6^*$; the choice of $P$ is itself a Structural Postulate. Isotropy alone does not select $H_3$, since $O_h$ and $T_d$ also satisfy macroscopic finite-point isotropy; nor does spin lifting select it, because every finite rotation subgroup of $SO(3)$ has a canonical Spin$(3)$ preimage. App USS records $A_5$ as the architectural premise H1 and derives exact conditional Schur-cover and McKay consequences [Tier 1 mathematics under that premise]; it does not prove point-group uniqueness, and identifying these consequences as one physical $E_8\to H_4\to\mathbb{Z}^6$ route is architectural compatibility, not a forcing theorem.

 * *Examples:* The **Electron Mass** chain is Tier 2 mechanism plus a Tier 3 exponent anchor ($n=-107$, pending O.14 selection) plus the Tier 4 O.14 physical-link conjecture; because $m_e$ is the A1 anchor, its numerical row is a Tier 3 anchor-normalised Planck-link check, whose 1006 ppm residual misses the pre-registered 1000 ppm pre-commitment (App FSM P.1c: FAIL of that rule). * **Tier 3 (Phenomenological / Calibrated):** Models calibrated to observation where a first-principles derivation is currently incomplete (e.g., requiring full non-perturbative simulation of defect ensembles or horizon-scale boundary conditions). Applies to any sector — biophysical, cosmological, or phenomenological — where free parameters are adjusted to match empirical anchors and a geometric derivation is pending. This tier also covers explicit conditional physical maps from a geometric invariant or coordinate diagnostic to a physical coupling or response coefficient: such a hypothesis need not be a continuous fitted parameter, but it remains an independent commitment until a normalization-invariant dynamical derivation closes it.
 * *Examples:* the stiffness-map hypothesis P3b, $K_\perp/K_\parallel=\phi^{-18}$, and its $\phi^{-9}$ and $\phi^{-36}$ descendants pending O.15(b); the electroweak boundary hypothesis H-EW; biological lag calculations ($\tau_{lag}$); Chiral Enhancement Factors in tubulin; the Consensus Decay Constant ($\lambda_c$, pending derivation from phason stiffness ratio $K_\perp/K_\parallel$, V1 Ch11 §11.2.4); the PMNS mixing angles (Tier 3; the bare $\theta_{23}=45^\circ$ lies inside the NuFIT 6.0 $3\sigma$ range with and without Super-Kamiokande data, and the octant is unresolved); the **Born Rule**, a Tier 3 conditional compatibility theorem pending O.40a/O.40b additivity and noncontextuality closure; the equation of state $w(z)_{bio}$ (sustained phantom phase asymptoting to $-1$ from below; the phantom direction itself is Tier 4, with no ghost-free action, V2 Ch14 §14.3.1; observational distinguishability threshold at $z \approx 0.28$ per V2 Ch14 §14.6.3).

* **Tier 3/4 (Straddle Label):** Tier 3 form / Tier 4 realization. The label marks a claim whose form is Tier 3 and whose realization is Tier 4; the text must say which part is which.

* **Tier 4 (Speculative / Exploratory):** Claims that are either (a) **exploratory calculations** where the dimensional structure is correct but the numerical coefficient is uncertain by >1 order of magnitude (with explicit OOM bounds), or (b) **ontological extensions** that lie outside the core falsifiable theory (identity persistence after biological termination, trans-temporal identity correlations), or (c) **evidential readings of numerical matches** whose evidential weight a preregistered control has not distinguished from numerology. Under (c) the arithmetic itself is not demoted: the computed value and its residual stay as descriptive arithmetic, and only their use as evidence that φ or icosahedral structure is special is Tier 4. No Tier 4 claim is used as support for a core result; where a core chain inherits a Tier 4 link (the O.14 physical-link conjecture, the phantom direction of the registered $w(z)$ channel), the chain carries that Tier 4 label.
 * *Examples (Exploratory):* Biogenic dark energy driving force magnitudes; neutrino mass scale absolute normalization.
 * *Examples (Ontological):* Identity persistence across bodies; consciousness continuity in reversible computational states.
 * *Examples (Evidential, (c)):* evidential readings of φ-formula numerical matches (App PSC §PSC.9.6): the lepton, proton, quark, Higgs-VEV, coupling, mixing-angle and Newton-$G$ matches, read as evidence that φ is special. The preregistered constant control test (v3, NEGATIVE-FRAGILE) found φ's pattern of matches on 21 constants not distinguishable from that of random constants; it "excludes, at power ≥ 0.8, an effect in which φ hits at least 6 of the 8 informative rows through a real effect; weaker effects are not excluded."
